## Module 4: Holistic Evaluation Framework (10 hours)

### 4.1 Academic Assessment
**Evaluation Criteria:**
- **Prior Academic Performance** (30%)
  - GPA and course rigor
  - Standardized test scores
  - Academic awards and honors
  
- **Core Competencies** (25%)
  - Critical thinking skills
  - Written communication
  - Research capabilities
  - Problem-solving abilities

- **Learning Potential** (25%)
  - Growth trajectory
  - Intellectual curiosity
  - Adaptability and resilience
  - Self-directed learning capacity

- **Academic Readiness** (20%)
  - Prerequisite knowledge
  - Study skills and habits
  - Time management
  - Technology proficiency

**Scoring Guidelines:**
```typescript
interface AcademicScore {
  priorPerformance: number; // 0-30 points
  coreCompetencies: number; // 0-25 points
  learningPotential: number; // 0-25 points
  academicReadiness: number; // 0-20 points
  totalScore: number; // 0-100 points
  recommendation: 'Highly Recommended' | 'Recommended' | 'Conditional' | 'Not Recommended';
}
```

### 4.2 Spiritual Evaluation
**Assessment Components:**
- **Personal Testimony** (25%)
  - Authenticity and clarity
  - Transformation evidence
  - Kingdom focus
  - Depth of understanding

- **Spiritual Maturity** (25%)
  - Prayer life and disciplines
  - Biblical knowledge
  - Fruit of the Spirit evidence
  - Response to challenges

- **Ministry Experience** (20%)
  - Service involvement
  - Leadership roles
  - Impact and effectiveness
  - Teachability and growth

- **Calling Clarity** (30%)
  - Divine purpose understanding
  - Ministry vision
  - Alignment with ScrollUniversity mission
  - Prophetic confirmation

**Red Flags to Watch:**
- Inconsistencies in testimony
- Lack of spiritual fruit evidence
- Unclear or misaligned calling
- Character concerns from references
- Spiritual pride or unteachability

### 4.3 Character Assessment
**Character Traits Evaluated:**
- Integrity and honesty
- Humility and teachability
- Servant leadership
- Perseverance and resilience
- Compassion and empathy
- Accountability and responsibility

**Reference Verification:**
1. Contact all listed references
2. Use standardized reference questionnaire
3. Probe for specific examples
4. Assess consistency across references
5. Follow up on any concerns

### 4.4 Interview Evaluation
**Interview Structure:**
- **Introduction** (5 minutes): Build rapport
- **Academic Discussion** (15 minutes): Assess intellectual capacity
- **Spiritual Journey** (20 minutes): Explore faith and calling
- **Character Assessment** (15 minutes): Evaluate integrity and values
- **Questions & Closing** (5 minutes): Address applicant concerns

**Interview Best Practices:**
- Create welcoming, non-threatening environment
- Ask open-ended questions
- Listen actively and take detailed notes
- Observe non-verbal communication
- Pray for discernment before and during interview
- Document impressions immediately after

## Module 5: Decision-Making Process (6 hours)

### 5.1 Committee Review Process
**Committee Composition:**
- Admissions Director (Chair)
- Academic Dean or Representative
- Spiritual Formation Director
- Faculty Representative
- Student Life Representative

**Review Meeting Structure:**
1. **Application Presentation** (10 minutes per applicant)
   - Admissions officer presents comprehensive profile
   - Highlights strengths and concerns
   - Shares interview impressions

2. **Committee Discussion** (15 minutes)
   - Members share perspectives
   - Address questions and concerns
   - Seek spiritual discernment

3. **Decision Voting** (5 minutes)
   - Each member votes: Accept, Conditional Accept, Waitlist, Deny
   - Chair facilitates consensus building
   - Final decision documented with rationale

### 5.2 Decision Categories
**Acceptance:**
- Strong academic and spiritual profile
- Clear calling alignment
- Positive character assessment
- Committee consensus

**Conditional Acceptance:**
- Strong overall profile with specific concerns
- Conditions may include:
  - Prerequisite coursework completion
  - Spiritual mentorship requirement
  - Academic probation monitoring
  - Character development plan

**Waitlist:**
- Qualified applicant, limited capacity
- May be offered admission if space becomes available
- Maintain regular communication
- Provide timeline expectations

**Denial:**
- Significant academic deficiencies
- Spiritual misalignment
- Character concerns
- Lack of readiness for university-level study

### 5.3 Decision Communication
**Acceptance Letters:**
- Congratulatory tone
- Next steps clearly outlined
- Enrollment deadline specified
- Financial aid information included
- Welcome to ScrollUniversity community

**Denial Letters:**
- Compassionate and respectful tone
- General reasons for decision (without excessive detail)
- Encouragement for future growth
- Alternative pathways suggested
- Appeal process information

## Module 6: Compliance and Ethics (4 hours)

### 6.1 Legal and Regulatory Compliance
**FERPA Compliance:**
- Protect applicant privacy
- Secure document handling
- Limited information sharing
- Proper consent for disclosures

**GDPR Compliance (International Applicants):**
- Data processing transparency
- Right to access and deletion
- Consent management
- Cross-border data transfer protocols

**Non-Discrimination:**
- Equal opportunity evaluation
- Prohibited discrimination factors
- Reasonable accommodations
- Accessibility compliance

### 6.2 Ethical Standards
**Conflict of Interest:**
- Disclose personal relationships with applicants
- Recuse from decisions involving conflicts
- Maintain professional boundaries
- Report ethical concerns

**Confidentiality:**
- Protect applicant information
- Secure document storage
- Limited access to sensitive data
- Professional discretion in discussions

**Integrity in Evaluation:**
- Objective and fair assessment
- Consistent application of criteria
- Documentation of decisions
- Transparency in process
