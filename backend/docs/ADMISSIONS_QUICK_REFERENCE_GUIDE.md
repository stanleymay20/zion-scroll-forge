## Application Processing Quick Steps

### Step 1: Initial Review (15 minutes)
```
1. Open application in system
2. Verify completeness:
   ✓ Personal information complete
   ✓ Application fee paid
   ✓ Required documents uploaded
   ✓ All sections completed
3. Update status to "Under Review"
4. Send acknowledgment email (if not automated)
```

### Step 2: Document Verification (30 minutes)
```
1. Review each document:
   ✓ Academic transcripts (official)
   ✓ Test scores (if applicable)
   ✓ Character references (minimum 3)
   ✓ Personal testimony
   ✓ Ministry experience documentation
2. Verify authenticity
3. Flag any concerns
4. Update document status
```

### Step 3: Eligibility Assessment (45 minutes)
```
1. Academic eligibility:
   ✓ High school diploma/equivalent
   ✓ Minimum GPA (2.5 or equivalent)
   ✓ Language proficiency
   ✓ Prerequisite courses
2. Spiritual eligibility:
   ✓ Salvation testimony
   ✓ Spiritual maturity indicators
   ✓ Church involvement
   ✓ Character references
3. Determine eligibility status
4. Communicate with applicant
```

### Step 4: Holistic Evaluation (2-3 hours)
```
1. Academic Assessment (use rubric):
   - Prior performance (0-30 points)
   - Core competencies (0-25 points)
   - Learning potential (0-25 points)
   - Academic readiness (0-20 points)
   
2. Spiritual Evaluation (use rubric):
   - Personal testimony (0-25 points)
   - Spiritual maturity (0-25 points)
   - Ministry experience (0-20 points)
   - Calling clarity (0-30 points)
   
3. Character Assessment:
   - Contact references
   - Complete assessment form
   - Document findings
   
4. Prepare comprehensive profile
```

## Evaluation Rubrics

### Academic Assessment Rubric

**Prior Performance (0-30 points)**
- 25-30: GPA 3.5+, rigorous coursework, academic honors
- 20-24: GPA 3.0-3.49, solid coursework, some recognition
- 15-19: GPA 2.5-2.99, adequate coursework
- 10-14: GPA 2.0-2.49, minimal coursework
- 0-9: GPA <2.0 or significant concerns

**Core Competencies (0-25 points)**
- 20-25: Exceptional critical thinking, communication, research
- 15-19: Strong competencies across all areas
- 10-14: Adequate competencies, some development needed
- 5-9: Weak competencies, significant development needed
- 0-4: Insufficient competencies

**Learning Potential (0-25 points)**
- 20-25: Exceptional growth trajectory, high curiosity
- 15-19: Strong potential, good adaptability
- 10-14: Adequate potential, moderate growth
- 5-9: Limited potential, slow growth
- 0-4: Minimal potential

**Academic Readiness (0-20 points)**
- 16-20: Fully prepared, strong study skills
- 11-15: Generally prepared, adequate skills
- 6-10: Somewhat prepared, needs support
- 1-5: Underprepared, significant support needed
- 0: Not ready for university-level study

### Spiritual Evaluation Rubric

**Personal Testimony (0-25 points)**
- 20-25: Authentic, clear, deep, transformative, kingdom-focused
- 15-19: Genuine, clear, good depth, evidence of transformation
- 10-14: Sincere, adequate clarity, some transformation
- 5-9: Unclear or shallow, limited transformation
- 0-4: Questionable authenticity or very unclear

**Spiritual Maturity (0-25 points)**
- 20-25: Exceptional prayer life, biblical knowledge, spiritual fruit
- 15-19: Strong spiritual disciplines and fruit
- 10-14: Adequate spiritual practices and growth
- 5-9: Limited spiritual disciplines or fruit
- 0-4: Minimal spiritual maturity

**Ministry Experience (0-20 points)**
- 16-20: Extensive service, leadership, significant impact
- 11-15: Good service involvement, some leadership
- 6-10: Some service experience, limited leadership
- 1-5: Minimal service involvement
- 0: No ministry experience

**Calling Clarity (0-30 points)**
- 25-30: Crystal clear calling, strong vision, prophetic confirmation
- 20-24: Clear calling, good vision, some confirmation
- 15-19: Emerging calling, developing vision
- 10-14: Unclear calling, vague vision
- 0-9: No clear calling or misaligned vision

## Interview Guide

### Pre-Interview (15 minutes)
- Review applicant file thoroughly
- Prepare specific questions based on application
- Test technology (for virtual interviews)
- Pray for discernment

### Interview Structure (60 minutes)

**Introduction (5 minutes)**
- Welcome and build rapport
- Explain interview purpose and structure
- Put applicant at ease

**Academic Discussion (15 minutes)**
- "Tell me about your academic journey."
- "What subjects excite you most and why?"
- "How do you approach challenging coursework?"
- "What are your academic goals?"

**Spiritual Journey (20 minutes)**
- "Share your testimony with me."
- "How has your faith shaped your life?"
- "Describe your spiritual disciplines."
- "What is God calling you to do?"
- "How does ScrollUniversity fit into that calling?"

**Character Assessment (15 minutes)**
- "Tell me about a time you faced a significant challenge."
- "How do you handle conflict or disagreement?"
- "Describe your leadership style."
- "What does integrity mean to you?"

**Questions & Closing (5 minutes)**
- "What questions do you have for me?"
- "Is there anything else you'd like me to know?"
- Thank applicant and explain next steps

### Post-Interview (15 minutes)
- Complete evaluation form immediately
- Document specific observations
- Rate on all criteria
- Provide overall recommendation

## Common Scenarios and Responses

### Scenario 1: Missing Documents
**Applicant asks:** "I submitted my transcript weeks ago. Why isn't it showing?"

**Response:** "Let me check your file. [Check system] I see we received your transcript on [date], and it's currently being verified. This process typically takes 3-5 business days. I'll follow up with our verification team and get back to you by [specific date]."

### Scenario 2: Low GPA Concern
**Applicant asks:** "My GPA is below 2.5. Can I still apply?"

**Response:** "We appreciate your interest in ScrollUniversity. While our typical minimum GPA is 2.5, we do consider the whole person. If you have extenuating circumstances, strong spiritual calling, or other compensating factors, we encourage you to apply and explain your situation in your personal statement. We also offer bridge programs for students who need additional academic preparation."

### Scenario 3: Interview Anxiety
**Applicant says:** "I'm really nervous about the interview."

**Response:** "That's completely understandable, and many applicants feel the same way. Remember, this is a conversation, not an interrogation. We want to get to know you better and understand your calling. Be yourself, be honest, and don't worry about having perfect answers. We're looking for authenticity, not perfection."

### Scenario 4: Decision Timeline
**Applicant asks:** "When will I hear about my decision?"

**Response:** "Based on when your application was completed, you can expect to hear from us by [specific date]. Our typical timeline is 4-6 weeks from application completion to decision notification. You can check your application status anytime through your applicant portal."

### Scenario 5: Financial Concerns
**Applicant asks:** "I can't afford tuition. Should I even apply?"

**Response:** "Please don't let financial concerns prevent you from applying. ScrollUniversity is committed to making education accessible to all called students. We offer scholarships, financial aid, payment plans, and our unique ScrollGold earning system. Once you're admitted, our financial aid office will work with you to create a funding plan."

## System Shortcuts and Tips
