### Decision-Making Protocols

**Protocol 001: Consensus Building**
- Used when committee members have differing opinions
- Structured discussion and re-voting process
- Chair makes final decision if no consensus

**Protocol 002: Tie-Breaking**
- Committee Chair casts deciding vote
- Escalation to Director if needed
- Waitlist as compromise option

**Protocol 003: Borderline Cases**
- Identify specific strengths and weaknesses
- Consider conditional admission
- Determine support services needed

**Protocol 004: Special Circumstances**
- Document unusual situations
- Consult relevant university offices
- Seek Director approval for exceptions

**Protocol 005: Conflict of Interest**
- Immediate disclosure required
- Recusal from evaluation and decision
- Independent review assignment

## Process Improvement Framework

### Improvement Methodology

**PDCA Cycle:**
1. **Plan**: Identify opportunities, analyze causes, define objectives
2. **Do**: Pilot implementation, train staff, monitor execution
3. **Check**: Measure results, analyze performance, gather feedback
4. **Act**: Standardize, adjust, document, share

**Six Sigma DMAIC:**
1. **Define**: Problem statement, scope, goals
2. **Measure**: Baseline data, key metrics
3. **Analyze**: Data patterns, root causes
4. **Improve**: Generate solutions, implement
5. **Control**: Standardize, monitor, sustain
