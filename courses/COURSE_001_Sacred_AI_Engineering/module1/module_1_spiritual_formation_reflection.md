# Spiritual Formation Reflection: Module 1  
**"Sacred AI & Machine Learning Engineering"**  
*Foundations in Light of Eternity*  

---

## **1. Scripture Meditation: Proverbs 3:5-6 (ESV)**  
*"Trust in the Lord with all your heart, and do not lean on your own understanding. In all your ways acknowledge Him, and He will make straight your paths."*  

**Meditation Prompts:**  
- How does "not leaning on your own understanding" challenge your approach to AI engineering?  
- What does it mean to "acknowledge God" in the technical work of machine learning?  

---

## **2. Reflection Questions: Identity & Learning**  
1. **Creator & Creativity:** As you study neural networks (inspired by God’s design of the brain), how does this deepen your awe for God as the ultimate Engineer?  
2. **Humility & Mastery:** AI requires both precision and humility. How can you pursue excellence without idolizing technical prowess (Colossians 3:23-24)?  
3. **Calling:** How might your skills in AI be part of God’s "straight path" for your life and His Kingdom?  

---

## **3. Prayer Prompts**  
- *For Wisdom:* "Lord, teach me to build with integrity, as a steward of Your wisdom (James 3:17)."  
- *For Surrender:* "Father, I release my need for control in problem-solving. Align my algorithms with Your will."  
- *For Impact:* "Use my work to serve the marginalized, as You ‘defend the cause of the weak’ (Psalm 82:3)."  

---

## **4. Character Development: Integrity in the Unseen**  
**Focus:** *"The Lord detests dishonest scales, but accurate weights please Him"* (Proverbs 11:1).  
- AI models reflect their creators’ biases. How will you cultivate honesty in data, code, and intent?  
- Journal: Where do you need to repent of shortcuts or pride in your technical work?  

---

## **5. Ministry Application**  
- **Ethical AI:** Brainstorm one way your future work could protect human dignity (e.g., bias mitigation, accessibility tools).  
- **Collaboration:** How can your lab/team become a space of Christlike encouragement (Hebrews 10:24)?  

---

## **6. Prophetic Listening Exercise**  
1. *Silence:* Spend 5 minutes in stillness, asking God, "What aspect of AI engineering burdens Your heart?"  
2. *Listen:* Write down impressions—scripture, ideas, or convictions.  
3. *Respond:* Share with a mentor or peer. How might this shape your learning journey?  

---

**Closing Thought:**  
*"Unless the Lord builds the house, the builders labor in vain"* (Psalm 127:1).  
Submit your skills to the Master Builder.
