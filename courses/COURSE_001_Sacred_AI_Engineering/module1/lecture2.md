## 2. DOWNLOAD: **Foundations of AI and Machine Learning**

### Key Concepts:
1. **Artificial Intelligence (AI):** A branch of computer science focused on creating systems that can perform tasks requiring human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, and problem-solving.
2. **Machine Learning (ML):** A subset of AI that enables systems to learn from data and improve performance without explicit programming.
3. **Neural Networks:** Computational models inspired by the human brain, used to process complex data patterns.
4. **Ethical AI Development:** Designing AI systems that are fair, transparent, and accountable, ensuring they align with biblical values.

### Examples and Analogies:

## 3. DEMONSTRATION: **Building a Fair AI Model for Loan Approval**

### Scenario:
A bank wants to use AI to approve loans but is concerned about bias against certain demographics. We’ll design a fair and transparent AI model.

### Step-by-Step Walkthrough:
1. **Data Collection:** Gather historical loan data, ensuring representation across demographics.
2. **Preprocessing:** Clean the data, removing irrelevant features and addressing missing values.
3. **Feature Engineering:** Identify key predictors (e.g., income, credit score) while avoiding biased variables (e.g., race).
4. **Model Selection:** Choose a decision tree algorithm for its interpretability.
5. **Training:** Train the model on 80% of the data, ensuring it learns fair patterns.
6. **Evaluation:** Test the model on the remaining 20%, using fairness metrics like "equalized odds" to ensure unbiased outcomes.
7. **Deployment:** Deploy the model with transparency, explaining its decision-making process to users.

### Real-World Case Study:
In 2019, Apple Card faced criticism for gender bias in credit limits. By following fair AI practices, such incidents can be prevented.
