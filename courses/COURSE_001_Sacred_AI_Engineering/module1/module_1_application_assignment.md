# Module 1 Assignment: Building a Kingdom-Focused AI Model

## **Objective**
The goal of this assignment is to apply foundational AI engineering principles to create a practical machine learning model that aligns with a kingdom perspective. You will develop a model that addresses a real-world problem while considering ethical implications and the potential for ministry or career impact.

---

## **Instructions**
1. **Problem Selection**:  
   Choose a real-world problem that aligns with a kingdom perspective (e.g., predicting food insecurity, identifying communities in need of resources, or classifying Scripture passages for thematic analysis).  
   Ensure the problem is measurable and has a clear societal or ministry impact.

2. **Data Collection**:  
   Identify and collect a dataset relevant to your problem. This could be from public datasets (e.g., Kaggle, government databases) or self-collected data.  
   Ensure the dataset is ethical, unbiased, and representative.

3. **Model Development**:  
   Use Python and libraries like Scikit-learn or TensorFlow to build a simple machine learning model (e.g., logistic regression, decision tree, or basic neural network).  
   Focus on understanding the data preprocessing, model training, and evaluation steps.

4. **Ethical Considerations**:  
   Reflect on the ethical implications of your model. How might it impact individuals or communities? What biases could arise, and how can they be mitigated?  
   Write a brief ethical analysis (1-2 paragraphs) as part of your report.

5. **Kingdom Impact**:  
   Describe how your model could be used for ministry or societal benefit. For example, could it help a nonprofit organization make better decisions? Could it support a church initiative?  
   Include this in your final report.

---

## **Project Deliverables**
1. **Code**:  
   A Python script or Jupyter Notebook containing your machine learning model. Include comments and documentation for clarity.

2. **Report**:  
   A 2-3 page report summarizing:
   - The problem you addressed and its kingdom relevance.
   - The dataset used and preprocessing steps.
   - The model architecture and evaluation metrics (e.g., accuracy, precision, recall).
   - Ethical considerations and kingdom impact.

3. **Presentation**:  
   A 5-minute presentation (slides or video) explaining your project, its technical aspects, and its potential ministry or career application.
