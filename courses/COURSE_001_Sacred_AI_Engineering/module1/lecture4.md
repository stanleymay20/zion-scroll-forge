### **2. DOWNLOAD: Ethical AI – Foundations & Biblical Mandates**  

#### **Key Concepts**  
1. **Bias & Fairness in AI**  
   - **Definition:** Bias occurs when an AI system produces systematically prejudiced results due to flawed assumptions in training data or design.  
   - **Example:** Amazon’s AI recruiting tool downgraded resumes with words like "women’s" (e.g., "women’s chess club").  
   - **Biblical Parallel:** *"Do not pervert justice; do not show partiality."* (Deuteronomy 16:19)  

2. **Transparency & Accountability**  
   - **"Black Box" Problem:** Many AI models (e.g., deep neural networks) are opaque, making it hard to audit decisions.  
   - **Kingdom Principle:** *"For there is nothing hidden that will not be disclosed."* (Luke 8:17)  
   - **Solution:** Explainable AI (XAI) techniques (e.g., SHAP values, LIME).  

3. **AI for the Vulnerable**  
   - **Case Study:** AI-powered loan approval systems often disadvantage low-income applicants.  
   - **Proverbs 31:8-9** – *"Speak up for those who cannot speak for themselves."*  

#### **Theological Framework: AI as Co-Creation**  
- **Stewardship Model:** AI should reflect God’s character—just, compassionate, and truthful.  
- **Warning Against Idolatry:** *"All who make idols are nothing, and the things they treasure are worthless."* (Isaiah 44:9)  
  - Are we trusting AI more than God’s wisdom?

### **3. DEMONSTRATION: Auditing a Biased Algorithm**  

**Scenario:** A university uses an AI system to screen scholarship applicants. You suspect it favors wealthier candidates.  

**Step-by-Step Audit:**  
1. **Data Inspection:**  
   - Check training data: Does it overrepresent affluent schools?  
   - **Example:** If 80% of past winners came from private schools, the AI may learn to favor them.  

2. **Bias Testing:**  
   - Use fairness metrics (e.g., demographic parity, equal opportunity).  
   - **Tool:** IBM’s AI Fairness 360 toolkit.  

3. **Mitigation Strategy:**  
   - Rebalance training data.  
   - Apply adversarial debiasing (a technique to reduce bias in models).  

**Biblical Integration:**  
- *"Do not exploit the poor because they are poor."* (Proverbs 22:22)  
- Ethical AI requires proactive justice, not passive neutrality.
