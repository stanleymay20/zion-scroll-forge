# Module 1: Foundations of Sacred AI & Machine Learning Engineering

## Module Title and Number  
**Module 1:** Foundations of Sacred AI & Machine Learning Engineering  

## Learning Objectives  
By the end of this module, students will be able to:  
1. Define artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) from both a technical and kingdom perspective.  
2. Explain the basic principles of neural networks and their role in AI development.  
3. Evaluate the ethical implications of AI through the lens of biblical stewardship.  
4. Apply foundational AI concepts to solve a simple real-world problem using Python and ML libraries.  
5. Articulate how AI can be used to advance God’s purposes in the world.  

## Key Concepts Covered  
- Introduction to AI and ML: Definitions, history, and kingdom perspective  
- Neural networks: Basic architecture and functioning  
- Ethical AI: Biblical principles for AI stewardship  
- Practical AI: Hands-on introduction to Python and ML libraries (e.g., TensorFlow, Scikit-learn)  

## Scripture Foundations  
1. **Genesis 1:28** - "Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the earth and subdue it."  
   *Reflection:* AI as a tool for stewardship and dominion in creation.  
2. **Proverbs 3:5-6** - "Trust in the Lord with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding."  
   *Reflection:* Balancing human innovation with reliance on divine wisdom in AI development.  
3. **Matthew 22:37-39** - "Love the Lord your God with all your heart... and love your neighbor as yourself."  
   *Reflection:* Ethical AI as an expression of love for God and neighbor.  

## Real-World Applications  
- Predictive analytics for humanitarian aid distribution  
- Ethical AI frameworks for healthcare decision-making  
- Neural networks in image recognition for conservation efforts  

## Module Structure  
**Lecture 1:** Introduction to AI and ML - Definitions, History, and Kingdom Perspective  
**Lecture 2:** Neural Networks - Architecture, Functioning, and Applications  
**Lecture 3:** Ethical AI - Biblical Principles and Modern Challenges  
**Lecture 4:** Practical AI - Hands-On Python and ML Libraries  

## Assessment Overview  
1. **Quiz:** Multiple-choice and short-answer questions on foundational AI concepts (20%)  
2. **Ethical Reflection Paper:** 500-word essay on biblical principles for AI stewardship (30%)  
3. **Coding Assignment:** Solve a simple ML problem using Python and TensorFlow (30%)  
4. **Class Participation:** Active engagement in discussions and group activities (20%)  

## Estimated Time Commitment  
- Lecture attendance: 4 hours  
- Reading and preparation: 6 hours  
- Coding assignment: 6 hours  
- Ethical reflection paper: 4 hours  
- Quiz preparation: 2 hours  
**Total:** 22 hours  

---

This module integrates revelation and reason by grounding technical AI concepts in biblical principles. It focuses on transformation by encouraging students to see AI as a tool for kingdom impact and includes progressive ascension elements by building foundational knowledge for advanced modules. Theory is tied to practice through hands-on coding assignments and real-world applications.
