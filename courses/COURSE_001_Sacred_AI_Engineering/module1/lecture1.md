## 2. DOWNLOAD: Core Concepts of Sacred AI  

### Key Concept 1: What is AI?  
**Definition**: Systems that perform tasks requiring human intelligence (e.g., learning, reasoning, perception).  
- **Example**: Netflix’s recommendation engine (Matthew 7:20—"By their fruits you will know them").  
- **Analogy**: AI as a "plow" (1 Corinthians 3:9—"God’s fellow workers"). Tools amplify intent.  

### Key Concept 2: Machine Learning (ML) Basics  
**Supervised Learning**: Labeled data → Predict outcomes (e.g., spam detection).  
- **Scripture**: Proverbs 4:7—"Get wisdom, and with all your getting, get understanding." Data requires discernment.  
**Unsupervised Learning**: Find patterns in unlabeled data (e.g., customer segmentation).  
- **Analogy**: Acts 17:26—God "determined the times set for them"—divine order in chaos.  

### Key Concept 3: Ethical AI Foundations  
- **Bias**: Amazon’s 2018 hiring AI discriminated against women (James 2:1—"Show no partiality").  
- **Transparency**: "Black box" vs. Daniel 5:12—"Light and understanding."  
- **Stewardship**: Psalm 24:1—"The earth is the Lord’s." Data is a trust.

## 3. DEMONSTRATION: Building a "Fruitful" AI Model  

### Case Study: Predicting Crop Yields for Food Justice  
**Problem**: 40% of food is wasted globally (Proverbs 13:23—"Injustice sweeps away food").  
**ML Solution**: Linear regression to predict harvests using weather, soil data.  

**Step-by-Step**:  
1. **Data Collection**: USDA datasets (e.g., rainfall, pH levels).  
2. **Preprocessing**: Handle missing values (Leviticus 19:35—"Just balances").  
3. **Training**: Fit model to historical yields.  
4. **Evaluation**: Mean Absolute Error (MAE) = 5%.  
5. **Deployment**: Farmers receive SMS alerts (Isaiah 58:10—"Feed the hungry").  

**Outcome**: Kenyan farms reduced waste by 22% (2022 pilot).
