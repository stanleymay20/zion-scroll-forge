# Module 5: Ethical Foundations of AI Development  
**Course:** Sacred AI & Machine Learning Engineering  
**Level:** Undergraduate  
**Category:** Technology  

## 1. Learning Objectives  
By the end of this module, students will:  
1. Analyze ethical frameworks (utilitarian, deontological, virtue-based) in AI decision-making.  
2. Evaluate bias mitigation techniques in training datasets using Python fairness libraries.  
3. Design an AI system proposal incorporating at least 3 biblical principles of justice.  
4. Debate AI ethics case studies through a "wisdom lens" (Proverbs 4:7).  
5. Implement a basic fairness-aware machine learning model (e.g., demographic parity classifier).  

## 2. Key Concepts  
- **Theological Foundations:** Imago Dei (Genesis 1:27), Stewardship (Psalm 24:1)  
- **Technical:** Bias-variance tradeoff, Fairness metrics (disparate impact, equalized odds)  
- **Ethical:** Algorithmic accountability, Transparency vs. proprietary concerns  
- **Societal:** AI for social good, Digital neighbor principle (Luke 10:25-37)  

## 3. Scripture Foundations  
- *"Do not pervert justice or show partiality"* (Deuteronomy 16:19) - Applied to dataset curation  
- *"The wisdom from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle..."* (James 3:17) - AI alignment criteria  

## 4. Real-World Applications  
- **Healthcare:** Auditing diagnostic AI for racial bias  
- **Criminal Justice:** Risk assessment algorithms in light of Micah 6:8  
- **Finance:** Ethical lending algorithms  

## 5. Module Structure (4 Lectures)  
**Lecture 5.1:** *Theology of Technology*  
- Case Study: Social media recommendation engines vs. Philippians 4:8  
- Lab: Annotate datasets with virtue ethics criteria  

**Lecture 5.2:** *Bias Detection Methods*  
- Technical Deep Dive: SHAP values for bias localization  
- Workshop: Audit a pretrained model using AIF360  

**Lecture 5.3:** *Ethical Design Patterns*  
- Sacred Architecture: Tabernacle design principles → modular AI systems  
- Group Activity: Redesign a facial recognition system for missional purposes  

**Lecture 5.4:** *Wisdom Implementation*  
- Guest Lecture: Christian AI practitioners in industry  
- Hackathon: Build a "proverbs-based" chatbot filter  

## 6. Assessments  
- **Devotional Reflection (20%):** 500-word essay on Proverbs 16:3 applied to model deployment  
- **Technical Project (40%):** Fairness audit report with code submission  
- **Ethical Debate (20%):** Role-play as AI ethicists vs. corporate stakeholders  
- **Peer Review (20%):** Evaluate classmates' system proposals using rubric  

## 7. Time Commitment  
- **Total Hours:** 40 (10/week)  
  - Lectures: 8 hours  
  - Scriptural study: 4 hours  
  - Coding labs: 12 hours  
  - Community engagement (discussion forums): 6 hours  
  - Assessment work: 10 hours  

> *"The prudent see danger and take refuge, but the simple keep going and pay the penalty."* (Proverbs 22:3) - Module theme verse on proactive ethics
