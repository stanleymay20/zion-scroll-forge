# Module 5 Assignment: Ethical AI for Kingdom Impact

## Objectives
1. Understand the ethical considerations in AI development.
2. Apply machine learning techniques to solve a real-world problem with a kingdom perspective.
3. Develop a practical AI solution that aligns with ethical principles and promotes societal well-being.
4. Reflect on the potential ministry and career applications of your project.

## Instructions
### Step 1: Problem Identification
Identify a real-world problem that can be addressed using machine learning. Consider issues related to social justice, community development, or ministry outreach. Examples include:
- Predicting homelessness trends to allocate resources effectively.
- Classifying social media posts to identify mental health crises.
- Detecting fraudulent activities in non-profit donations.

### Step 2: Data Collection
Gather relevant data for your chosen problem. Use publicly available datasets or create your own through surveys or web scraping. Ensure the data is ethical and respects privacy.

### Step 3: Model Development
Develop a machine learning model to address the problem. Use Python and libraries such as Scikit-learn, TensorFlow, or PyTorch. Ensure your model is transparent and interpretable.

### Step 4: Ethical Evaluation
Evaluate your model from an ethical perspective. Consider biases, fairness, and potential societal impact. Document your findings and propose improvements if necessary.

### Step 5: Kingdom Impact Reflection
Write a reflection (500-700 words) on how your project can contribute to kingdom values and societal well-being. Discuss potential ministry or career applications.

## Real-World Application Project
Create a practical AI solution that addresses a real-world problem with a kingdom perspective. Your project should include:
- A clear problem statement.
- A dataset used for training and testing.
- A developed machine learning model.
- Ethical evaluation and reflection.
