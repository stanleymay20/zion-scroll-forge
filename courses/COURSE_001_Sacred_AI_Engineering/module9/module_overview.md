# Module 9: Sacred AI Engineering - Ethics, Accountability, and Kingdom Impact  

## Module Title and Number  
**Module 9:** Ethics, Accountability, and Kingdom Impact in AI Engineering  

## Learning Objectives  
By the end of this module, students will be able to:  
1. Articulate biblical principles for ethical AI development and deployment.  
2. Evaluate AI systems for potential biases, risks, and societal impacts from a kingdom perspective.  
3. Design accountability frameworks for AI systems that align with Christian values.  
4. Propose practical strategies for leveraging AI to advance justice, compassion, and stewardship in society.  
5. Reflect on personal responsibility as an AI engineer in shaping technology for God’s glory.  

## Key Concepts Covered  
- Biblical ethics and their application to AI  
- Bias, fairness, and justice in machine learning models  
- Accountability and transparency in AI systems  
- Stewardship of technology for societal good  
- The role of AI in advancing kingdom values (justice, mercy, love)  

## Scripture Foundations  
1. **Micah 6:8** - "He has shown you, O mortal, what is good. And what does the Lord require of you? To act justly and to love mercy and to walk humbly with your God."  
2. **Proverbs 4:7** - "The beginning of wisdom is this: Get wisdom. Though it cost all you have, get understanding."  
3. **Colossians 3:23** - "Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord, not for human masters."  

## Real-World Applications  
- Developing AI systems that promote fairness in hiring and lending practices.  
- Designing transparent AI models for healthcare to ensure equitable treatment.  
- Implementing accountability mechanisms in autonomous systems to prevent misuse.  
- Leveraging AI to address global challenges such as poverty, education, and disaster response.  

## Module Structure  
**Lecture 1:** Biblical Foundations for Ethical AI  
- Explore biblical principles of justice, mercy, and stewardship in technology.  

**Lecture 2:** Bias and Fairness in Machine Learning  
- Examine sources of bias in AI systems and strategies to mitigate them.  

**Lecture 3:** Accountability and Transparency in AI  
- Discuss frameworks for ensuring AI systems are transparent and accountable.  

**Lecture 4:** AI for Kingdom Impact  
- Explore case studies of AI applications advancing justice, compassion, and stewardship.  

## Assessment Overview  
1. **Reflection Paper (30%)** - Write a 3-page reflection on how biblical ethics should guide AI development.  
2. **Case Study Analysis (40%)** - Analyze an AI system for biases and propose ethical improvements.  
3. **Group Project (30%)** - Design an AI application that aligns with kingdom values and present it to the class.  

## Estimated Time Commitment  
- Lectures and readings: 8 hours  
- Reflection paper: 4 hours  
- Case study analysis: 6 hours  
- Group project: 8 hours  
**Total:** 26 hours  

This module integrates **revelation + reason** by grounding AI ethics in Scripture while applying technical rigor. It focuses on **transformation over information** by challenging students to align their work with kingdom values. The **progressive ascension** approach builds from foundational ethics to practical applications. Finally, it **ties theory to practice** through real-world case studies and a hands-on group project.
