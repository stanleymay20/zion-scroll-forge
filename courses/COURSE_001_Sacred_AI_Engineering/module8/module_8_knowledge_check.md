# **Module 8 Knowledge Check: Sacred AI & Machine Learning Engineering**  

## **Multiple Choice Questions (10 Questions)**  

### **1. What is the primary goal of supervised learning?**  
A) To discover hidden patterns in unlabeled data  
B) To predict outcomes based on labeled training data  
C) To optimize decision-making without human intervention  
D) To generate synthetic data for testing  

**Answer:** **B**  
**Explanation:** Supervised learning relies on labeled datasets to train models to predict outcomes. Unlike unsupervised learning (A), it requires explicit input-output pairs.  

---  

### **2. Which biblical principle aligns with the concept of ethical AI transparency?**  
A) "Do not bear false witness" (Exodus 20:16)  
B) "Love your neighbor as yourself" (Mark 12:31)  
C) "The truth will set you free" (John 8:32)  
D) All of the above  

**Answer:** **D**  
**Explanation:** Ethical AI requires honesty (A), care for users (B), and transparency (C) to prevent deception and harm.  

---  

### **3. What is a key limitation of neural networks?**  
A) They require minimal computational power  
B) They are highly interpretable  
C) They need large amounts of training data  
D) They cannot perform regression tasks  

**Answer:** **C**  
**Explanation:** Neural networks often require vast datasets for effective training, unlike simpler models. Their "black-box" nature also makes them less interpretable (B is false).  

---  

### **4. Which AI ethics principle is violated if a model disproportionately harms marginalized groups?**  
A) Justice  
B) Stewardship  
C) Accountability  
D) Fairness  

**Answer:** **D**  
**Explanation:** Fairness ensures AI does not discriminate. Justice (A) is broader, while stewardship (B) and accountability (C) relate to responsibility.  

---  

### **5. What does "overfitting" mean in machine learning?**  
A) The model performs well on training data but poorly on unseen data  
B) The model generalizes too well to new datasets  
C) The model ignores important features  
D) The model requires less data  

**Answer:** **A**  
**Explanation:** Overfitting occurs when a model memorizes training data instead of learning generalizable patterns.  

---  

### **6. How does Proverbs 4:7 ("Wisdom is the principal thing") apply to AI development?**  
A) Prioritize profit over ethical concerns  
B) Seek understanding before deploying AI systems  
C) Use AI to replace human judgment entirely  
D) Focus only on technical performance  

**Answer:** **B**  
**Explanation:** Wisdom in AI means careful, ethical development rather than hasty or exploitative practices.  

---  

### **7. Which technique helps prevent bias in AI datasets?**  
A) Using only historical data  
B) Ensuring diverse representation in training data  
C) Ignoring demographic variables  
D) Relying on a single data source  

**Answer:** **B**  
**Explanation:** Diverse data reduces bias, whereas A, C, and D can perpetuate or worsen it.  

---  

### **8. What is the role of loss functions in training ML models?**  
A) To measure prediction error and guide optimization  
B) To generate synthetic training samples  
C) To visualize neural network layers  
D) To label data automatically  

**Answer:** **A**  
**Explanation:** Loss functions quantify errors, helping models adjust weights during training.

### **9. Which biblical concept supports the idea of AI accountability?**  
A) "Each of us will give an account of ourselves to God" (Romans 14:12)  
B) "The heart is deceitful above all things" (Jeremiah 17:9)  
C) "Let your light shine before others" (Matthew 5:16)  
D) "Do not worry about tomorrow" (Matthew 6:34)

### **10. Why is cross-validation important in model evaluation?**  
A) It reduces the need for testing data  
B) It ensures the model generalizes well across different data splits  
C) It eliminates the need for hyperparameter tuning  
D) It guarantees 100% accuracy  

**Answer:** **B**  
**Explanation:** Cross-validation assesses model robustness by testing performance on multiple data subsets.

## **True/False Questions (5 Questions)**  

### **1. AI can replace human moral judgment entirely.**  
**Answer:** **False**  
**Reasoning:** Scripture emphasizes human responsibility (Proverbs 3:5-6). AI lacks moral agency and should assist, not replace, ethical decision-making.  

---  

### **2. Neural networks always outperform traditional ML models.**  
**Answer:** **False**  
**Reasoning:** Simpler models (e.g., linear regression) can be more efficient and interpretable (Ecclesiastes 7:29—"God made mankind upright").  

---  

### **3. Ethical AI requires considering long-term societal impacts.**  
**Answer:** **True**  
**Reasoning:** Biblical stewardship (Genesis 1:28) calls for responsible innovation that benefits future generations.  

---  

### **4. Data privacy is irrelevant in AI development.**  
**Answer:** **False**  
**Reasoning:** Respecting privacy aligns with "love your neighbor" (Mark 12:31) and protects individuals from harm.  

---  

### **5. AI fairness means treating all groups identically.**  
**Answer:** **False**  
**Reasoning:** True fairness may require equitable (not identical) treatment (Proverbs 31:8-9—"Speak up for those who cannot speak for themselves").
