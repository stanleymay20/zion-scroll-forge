## 2. DOWNLOAD  

### Key Concepts:  

#### Ethical AI Development  
Ethical AI development involves creating systems that are fair, transparent, accountable, and aligned with human values. It requires addressing biases, ensuring privacy, and considering the societal impact of AI technologies.  

#### Bias in AI  
Bias in AI occurs when a system produces unfair or discriminatory outcomes due to skewed data or flawed algorithms. For example, facial recognition systems have been shown to misidentify individuals with darker skin tones more frequently than those with lighter skin tones.  

#### Transparency and Explainability  
AI systems should be transparent in their decision-making processes. Explainability ensures that users can understand how and why a system arrived at a particular decision.  

#### Accountability  
AI developers and organizations must take responsibility for the societal impact of their technologies. This includes addressing unintended consequences and ensuring that AI systems are used ethically.  

### Examples and Analogies:  
Think of AI as a gardener. Just as a gardener must ensure that the soil is fertile and free of toxins, AI engineers must ensure that the data and algorithms used are free of bias and harmful influences.

## 3. DEMONSTRATION  

### Case Study: Amazon’s Hiring Algorithm  
In 2018, Amazon scrapped an AI hiring tool after discovering it was biased against women. The algorithm was trained on resumes submitted to the company over a 10-year period, most of which came from men. As a result, the system penalized resumes that included words like “women’s” or references to all-women’s colleges.  

### Step-by-Step Walkthrough:  

1. **Problem Identification**: Amazon’s AI hiring tool was producing biased outcomes.  
2. **Root Cause Analysis**: The training data was skewed toward male applicants.  
3. **Solution**: The company retrained the algorithm with a more balanced dataset and introduced fairness checks.  
4. **Outcome**: The tool was eventually discontinued, highlighting the importance of ethical AI development.  

### Lessons Learned:  
This case underscores the need for diverse training data, ongoing monitoring, and a commitment to fairness in AI systems.
