# Module 8 Assignment: Ethical AI for Social Good  
**Course:** Sacred AI & Machine Learning Engineering  
**Level:** Undergraduate  
**Estimated Time:** 5–10 hours  

---

## **Objectives**  
By completing this assignment, you will:  
1. Apply machine learning (ML) to solve a real-world problem with ethical considerations.  
2. Design an AI solution aligned with biblical principles of justice, compassion, and stewardship.  
3. Demonstrate technical proficiency in data preprocessing, model training, and evaluation.  
4. Reflect on the societal and spiritual impact of your project.  

---

## **Instructions**  

### **Step 1: Problem Selection**  
Choose **one** of the following focus areas (or propose your own with instructor approval):  
- **Poverty Alleviation:** Predict food insecurity hotspots using public datasets (e.g., World Bank, UN).  
- **Healthcare Access:** Build a classifier to prioritize telemedicine requests in underserved regions.  
- **Ethical Content Moderation:** Train a model to detect hate speech while preserving free speech.  

### **Step 2: Technical Implementation**  
1. **Data Collection & Preprocessing**  
   - Source a dataset (e.g., Kaggle, government portals, or APIs like Twitter for hate speech).  
   - Clean data (handle missing values, biases) and document ethical risks.  

2. **Model Development**  
   - Train a model (e.g., logistic regression, decision tree, or simple neural network) using Python (scikit-learn/TensorFlow).  
   - Evaluate performance (accuracy, precision, recall) and justify your metric choices.  

3. **Ethical Audit**  
   - Write a 1-page analysis: How could your model harm vulnerable groups? How would you mitigate this?  

### **Step 3: Kingdom Impact Reflection**  
Answer in 300–500 words:  
- How does your project reflect Matthew 25:40 (“Whatever you did for the least of these…”)?  
- How could this tool be used by churches or ministries?  

---

## **Deliverables**  
1. **Code Submission** (Jupyter Notebook or GitHub repo):  
   - Clean, commented code with a README explaining setup.  
2. **Report (PDF):**  
   - Problem statement, methodology, results, and ethical analysis.  
   - Kingdom impact reflection.  
3. **5-Minute Video Demo** (optional bonus):  
   - Showcase your model’s functionality and discuss its societal value.
