# Module 6 Assignment: Ethical AI for Kingdom Impact  

**Course:** Sacred AI & Machine Learning Engineering  
**Level:** Undergraduate  

---

## **Assignment Objectives**  
By the end of this assignment, students will:  
1. Understand the ethical considerations in AI development from a kingdom perspective.  
2. Apply machine learning techniques to solve a real-world problem with measurable outcomes.  
3. Design a practical AI solution that aligns with kingdom values and has a positive societal impact.  
4. Reflect on how AI can be used as a tool for ministry and career advancement.  

---

## **Detailed Instructions**  
You will design and implement a machine learning model to address a real-world problem that aligns with kingdom values. The problem should be relevant to your community, ministry, or future career.  

### **Steps:**  
1. **Problem Selection (1 hour):**  
   - Identify a problem in your community or ministry that can be addressed using AI (e.g., predicting homelessness risk, optimizing church resource allocation, detecting harmful online content).  
   - Write a brief problem statement (200 words) explaining the issue and how AI can help.  

2. **Data Collection and Preparation (2 hours):**  
   - Collect or simulate a dataset relevant to your problem.  
   - Preprocess the data (cleaning, normalization, feature engineering).  

3. **Model Development (3 hours):**  
   - Choose a machine learning algorithm (e.g., linear regression, decision trees, neural networks).  
   - Train and evaluate your model using appropriate metrics (e.g., accuracy, precision, recall).  

4. **Ethical and Kingdom Reflection (1 hour):**  
   - Reflect on the ethical implications of your AI solution.  
   - Write a 300-word reflection on how your project aligns with kingdom values and contributes to societal good.  

5. **Presentation and Documentation (2 hours):**  
   - Prepare a 5-minute presentation summarizing your project.  
   - Submit a detailed report (3-5 pages) including problem statement, methodology, results, and reflection.  

---

## **Real-World Application Project**  
Example Project:  
**Title:** Predicting At-Risk Youth for Mentorship Programs  
**Description:** Develop a machine learning model to identify youth in your community who are at risk of dropping out of school or engaging in harmful behaviors. Use demographic, academic, and behavioral data to predict risk levels.
