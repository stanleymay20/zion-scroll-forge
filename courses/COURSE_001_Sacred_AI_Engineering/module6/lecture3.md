## **3. DEMONSTRATION: Building a Fair Loan Approval Model**  

### **Problem Statement**  
A bank uses AI to approve loans, but historical data shows bias against minority applicants.  

### **Step-by-Step Ethical AI Implementation**  
1. **Audit the Data**  
   - Check for disparities in approval rates by race/gender.  
   - Use *disparate impact ratio*: `(Minority Approval Rate) / (Majority Approval Rate)`.  

2. **Pre-process for Fairness**  
   - Re-weight training data (e.g., boosting minority-representative samples).  
   - Apply *adversarial debiasing* to remove sensitive attribute influence.  

3. **Post-hoc Explainability**  
   - Use SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) to show which features influenced decisions.  

4. **Continuous Monitoring**  
   - Deploy fairness metrics (e.g., *equalized odds*) in production.  

**Scriptural Checkpoint:** *"Whoever can be trusted with little can be trusted with much"* (Luke 16:10).
