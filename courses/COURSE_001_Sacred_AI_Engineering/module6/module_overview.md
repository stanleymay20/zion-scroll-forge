# Module 6: Ethical AI Development & Kingdom Impact

### Module Title and Number  
**Module 6:** Ethical AI Development & Kingdom Impact  

---

### Learning Objectives  
By the end of this module, students will be able to:  
1. **Evaluate** the ethical implications of AI systems through the lens of biblical principles.  
2. **Design** AI solutions that prioritize fairness, accountability, and transparency.  
3. **Apply** machine learning models to real-world problems that align with kingdom values.  
4. **Reflect** on the transformative potential of AI in advancing justice, compassion, and stewardship.  

---

### Key Concepts Covered  
- Ethical frameworks for AI development  
- Bias detection and mitigation in machine learning  
- Transparency and explainability in AI systems  
- The role of AI in promoting justice and stewardship  
- Kingdom-centered innovation in technology  

---

### Scripture Foundations  
- **Micah 6:8** (ESV): "He has told you, O man, what is good; and what does the Lord require of you but to do justice, and to love kindness, and to walk humbly with your God?"  
- **Proverbs 4:7** (NIV): "The beginning of wisdom is this: Get wisdom. Though it cost all you have, get understanding."  
- **Colossians 3:23** (NIV): "Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord, not for human masters."  

---

### Real-World Applications  
- Developing AI tools to reduce bias in hiring processes  
- Creating transparent AI systems for healthcare decision-making  
- Using AI to address global challenges such as poverty and climate change  

---

### Module Structure  
**Lecture 1:** Foundations of Ethical AI  
- Explore ethical dilemmas in AI development.  
- Introduce biblical principles for ethical decision-making.  

**Lecture 2:** Bias and Fairness in Machine Learning  
- Understand sources of bias in data and algorithms.  
- Learn techniques for bias detection and mitigation.  

**Lecture 3:** Transparency and Accountability in AI  
- Study methods for making AI systems explainable.  
- Discuss accountability frameworks for AI developers.  

**Lecture 4:** AI for Kingdom Impact  
- Explore case studies of AI advancing justice and stewardship.  
- Brainstorm kingdom-centered AI projects.  

---

### Assessment Overview  
1. **Case Study Analysis (30%)**: Analyze an AI system for ethical implications and propose improvements.  
2. **Bias Mitigation Project (30%)**: Apply bias detection techniques to a dataset and present findings.  
3. **Reflective Essay (20%)**: Reflect on the role of AI in advancing kingdom values.  
4. **Group Presentation (20%)**: Propose a kingdom-centered AI solution to a real-world problem.  

---

### Estimated Time Commitment  
- **Lectures & Discussions:** 8 hours  
- **Readings & Assignments:** 6 hours  
- **Project Work:** 10 hours  
**Total:** 24 hours  

---

This module integrates revelation and reason, focusing on transforming students into ethical AI practitioners who leverage technology for kingdom impact. Through progressive ascension, students move from understanding ethical principles to applying them in practical, impactful ways.
