## **2. DOWNLOAD: Concept Teaching**  

### Key Concepts:  
1. **Ethical AI Development**: The process of designing, building, and deploying AI systems that align with moral principles, societal values, and biblical ethics.  
2. **Bias and Fairness**: Ensuring AI systems do not perpetuate or amplify existing biases, leading to unjust outcomes.  
3. **Transparency and Accountability**: Making AI decision-making processes understandable and holding developers responsible for their systems’ impacts.  
4. **Kingdom Perspective**: Viewing AI development as a form of stewardship, where technology serves humanity and glorifies God.

## **3. DEMONSTRATION: Worked Example**  

### Case Study: Bias in AI-Powered Hiring Systems  
**Scenario**: A company uses an AI system to screen job applicants. The algorithm is trained on historical hiring data, which predominantly favors male candidates for technical roles.  

**Step-by-Step Walkthrough**:  
1. **Identify the Problem**: The AI system perpetuates gender bias, disadvantaging qualified female candidates.  
2. **Analyze the Data**: Examine the training dataset to identify imbalances and biases.  
3. **Mitigate Bias**:  
   - Rebalance the dataset to include more examples of successful female candidates.  
   - Use fairness metrics to evaluate the algorithm’s performance across different demographic groups.  
   - Implement a post-processing step to adjust predictions for fairness.  
4. **Test and Validate**: Ensure the revised system performs fairly across all groups without sacrificing accuracy.  
5. **Deploy with Transparency**: Communicate to stakeholders how the system works and the steps taken to ensure fairness.  

**Outcome**: The revised hiring system promotes diversity and equity, reflecting biblical principles of justice and fairness.

## **4. ACTIVATION: Student Practice**  

### Hands-On Exercise: Designing a Fair AI System  
**Task**: You are tasked with designing an AI-powered loan approval system for a bank. The goal is to ensure fairness across different demographic groups while maintaining accuracy.  

**Steps**:  
1. **Dataset Analysis**: Examine the loan dataset for potential biases (e.g., race, gender, income level).  
2. **Fairness Metrics**: Choose fairness metrics (e.g., demographic parity, equal opportunity) to evaluate the system.  
3. **Model Training**: Train a machine learning model using techniques to mitigate bias (e.g., adversarial debiasing, reweighting).  
4. **Testing**: Evaluate the model’s performance on fairness metrics and accuracy.  
5. **Reflection**: Write a brief report on the steps taken to ensure fairness and how this aligns with biblical principles of justice.  

**Deliverable**: Submit your code, fairness evaluation results, and reflection report.
