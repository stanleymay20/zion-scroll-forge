## **Multiple Choice Questions**  
1. **What is the primary purpose of a neural network in machine learning?**  
   a) To store large amounts of data  
   b) To mimic the human brain's decision-making process  
   c) To replace human engineers  
   d) To perform mathematical calculations faster  
   **Answer:** b) To mimic the human brain's decision-making process  
   **Explanation:** Neural networks are designed to simulate the way the human brain processes information, enabling machines to learn and make decisions.

2. **Which of the following best describes supervised learning?**  
   a) Learning from unlabeled data  
   b) Learning from labeled data with known outputs  
   c) Learning without human intervention  
   d) Learning through trial and error  
   **Answer:** b) Learning from labeled data with known outputs  
   **Explanation:** Supervised learning involves training a model on data where the correct output is already known, allowing the model to learn patterns and make predictions.

3. **How does ethical AI development align with biblical principles?**  
   a) By prioritizing profit over people  
   b) By ensuring fairness, justice, and respect for human dignity  
   c) By avoiding transparency in decision-making  
   d) By ignoring societal impact  
   **Answer:** b) By ensuring fairness, justice, and respect for human dignity  
   **Explanation:** Biblical principles emphasize fairness, justice, and respect for all individuals, which should guide ethical AI development.

4. **What is a key challenge in deploying AI systems in real-world applications?**  
   a) Lack of computational power  
   b) Ensuring transparency and accountability  
   c) Over-reliance on human input  
   d) Limited availability of data  
   **Answer:** b) Ensuring transparency and accountability  
   **Explanation:** Transparency and accountability are critical to ensure AI systems are trustworthy and do not cause harm.

5. **Which biblical concept is most relevant to the idea of stewardship in AI development?**  
   a) Dominion over creation  
   b) Predestination  
   c) Grace  
   d) Redemption  
   **Answer:** a) Dominion over creation  
   **Explanation:** The concept of stewardship involves responsibly managing God's creation, including the development and use of AI technologies.

6. **What is the role of a loss function in machine learning?**  
   a) To measure the accuracy of a model's predictions  
   b) To store training data  
   c) To visualize data distributions  
   d) To optimize computational efficiency  
   **Answer:** a) To measure the accuracy of a model's predictions  
   **Explanation:** A loss function quantifies the difference between predicted and actual values, guiding the model's learning process.

7. **Which of the following is an example of unsupervised learning?**  
   a) Predicting house prices based on historical data  
   b) Grouping customers based on purchasing behavior  
   c) Classifying emails as spam or not spam  
   d) Training a chatbot to answer customer queries  
   **Answer:** b) Grouping customers based on purchasing behavior  
   **Explanation:** Unsupervised learning involves finding patterns in data without predefined labels, such as clustering.

8. **How can AI developers ensure their work honors God?**  
   a) By focusing solely on technical excellence  
   b) By prioritizing ethical considerations and societal impact  
   c) By avoiding collaboration with non-believers  
   d) By ignoring potential risks  
   **Answer:** b) By prioritizing ethical considerations and societal impact  
   **Explanation:** Honoring God involves using AI responsibly to benefit society and uphold ethical standards.

9. **What is the significance of bias in AI models?**  
   a) It improves model accuracy  
   b) It ensures fairness in decision-making  
   c) It can lead to unjust outcomes if not addressed  
   d) It is irrelevant in AI development  
   **Answer:** c) It can lead to unjust outcomes if not addressed  
   **Explanation:** Bias in AI models can perpetuate inequalities and harm marginalized groups if not properly mitigated.

10. **Which biblical verse emphasizes the importance of wisdom in technology development?**  
    a) Proverbs 3:5-6  
    b) Matthew 6:33  
    c) James 1:5  
    d) Philippians 4:13  
    **Answer:** c) James 1:5  
    **Explanation:** James 1:5 encourages seeking wisdom, which is essential for ethical and thoughtful AI development.
