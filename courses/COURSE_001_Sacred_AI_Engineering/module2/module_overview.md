# Module 2: Foundations of Machine Learning with a Kingdom Perspective  

## Module Title and Number  
**Module Title:** Foundations of Machine Learning with a Kingdom Perspective  
**Module Number:** Module 2  

---

## Learning Objectives  
By the end of this module, students will be able to:  
1. **Explain** the core principles of machine learning (ML) and its role in solving real-world problems from a kingdom worldview.  
2. **Design** simple ML models using supervised and unsupervised learning techniques.  
3. **Evaluate** the ethical implications of ML algorithms in light of biblical principles.  
4. **Apply** ML concepts to solve a real-world problem that aligns with kingdom values (e.g., poverty alleviation, healthcare improvement).  
5. **Integrate** Scripture-based wisdom into the development and deployment of ML systems.  

---

## Key Concepts Covered  
- Introduction to Machine Learning: Definitions, types (supervised, unsupervised, reinforcement learning).  
- The ML workflow: Data collection, preprocessing, model training, evaluation, and deployment.  
- Ethical considerations in ML: Bias, fairness, transparency, and accountability.  
- Kingdom perspective: Stewardship, justice, and love in AI development.  
- Practical ML tools: Introduction to Python libraries (e.g., Scikit-learn, TensorFlow).  

---

## Scripture Foundations  
1. **Proverbs 2:6** (NIV): *"For the Lord gives wisdom; from his mouth come knowledge and understanding."*  
   - Reflects the importance of seeking divine wisdom in the pursuit of knowledge, including ML engineering.  
2. **Micah 6:8** (NIV): *"He has shown you, O mortal, what is good. And what does the Lord require of you? To act justly and to love mercy and to walk humbly with your God."*  
   - Encourages ethical and just practices in ML development and deployment.  
3. **Colossians 3:23** (NIV): *"Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord, not for human masters."*  
   - Motivates students to approach ML engineering with excellence and integrity.  

---

## Real-World Applications  
- **Healthcare:** Using ML to predict disease outbreaks and improve patient care.  
- **Agriculture:** Applying ML to optimize crop yields and reduce food waste.  
- **Social Justice:** Leveraging ML to identify and address systemic biases in hiring practices.  
- **Disaster Relief:** Deploying ML models to predict and respond to natural disasters.  

---

## Module Structure (4 Lectures)  
1. **Lecture 1: Introduction to Machine Learning and Kingdom Perspectives**  
   - Overview of ML concepts and their alignment with kingdom values.  
   - Case studies of ML applications in kingdom-aligned projects.  

2. **Lecture 2: Supervised and Unsupervised Learning**  
   - Detailed exploration of ML types and their workflows.  
   - Hands-on lab: Building a simple ML model using Scikit-learn.  

3. **Lecture 3: Ethical Considerations in ML**  
   - Discussion on bias, fairness, and transparency in ML systems.  
   - Group activity: Analyzing ethical dilemmas in ML case studies.  

4. **Lecture 4: Applying ML to Kingdom-Centered Problems**  
   - Project-based learning: Students design an ML solution for a kingdom-aligned challenge (e.g., poverty reduction).  
   - Reflection on integrating biblical principles into ML engineering.  

---

## Assessment Overview  
1. **Weekly Quizzes (20%)**: Short quizzes to test understanding of ML concepts and ethical principles.  
2. **Ethical Case Study Analysis (20%)**: Group presentation on an ethical dilemma in ML.  
3. **ML Project (40%)**: Design and implement a simple ML model to address a kingdom-aligned problem.  
4. **Reflection Paper (20%)**: Write a 2-page reflection on how Scripture informs your approach to ML engineering.  

---

## Estimated Time Commitment  
- **Lectures and Readings:** 8 hours  
- **Hands-on Labs and Projects:** 10 hours  
- **Assessments and Reflection:** 6 hours  
**Total:** 24 hours
