# Module 2 Assignment: Ethical AI Development for Social Impact

## **Assignment Overview**
In this assignment, you will design and implement a practical machine learning model to address a real-world social issue, integrating ethical considerations and a kingdom perspective. The goal is to apply foundational AI engineering skills while prioritizing fairness, transparency, and positive societal impact.

---

## **Objectives**
By completing this assignment, you will:
1. Apply machine learning concepts to solve a real-world problem.
2. Develop an understanding of ethical AI principles and their importance.
3. Create a project that aligns with kingdom values, such as justice, compassion, and stewardship.
4. Gain hands-on experience in AI model development and evaluation.

---

## **Instructions**
1. **Identify a Problem**: Choose a social issue (e.g., poverty, education inequality, healthcare access) that can be addressed using machine learning. Explain why this issue matters and how AI can contribute to a solution.
2. **Data Collection**: Find or create a dataset relevant to your chosen problem. Ensure the data is ethical, unbiased, and representative of the population you aim to serve.
3. **Model Development**: Build a machine learning model (e.g., classification, regression, or clustering) using Python and libraries like scikit-learn or TensorFlow. Document your process, including data preprocessing, model selection, and training.
4. **Ethical Considerations**: Analyze potential biases or ethical concerns in your model. Propose strategies to mitigate these issues, such as fairness metrics, explainability tools, or diverse data sources.
5. **Kingdom Impact**: Reflect on how your project aligns with kingdom values. How does your work promote justice, compassion, or stewardship? Include a brief written reflection (200-300 words).
6. **Presentation**: Prepare a 5-minute presentation summarizing your project, including the problem, solution, ethical considerations, and kingdom impact.

---

## **Real-World Application Project**
You will develop a machine learning model to address a specific social issue. Examples include:
- Predicting food insecurity in underserved communities.
- Identifying students at risk of dropping out of school.
- Improving access to healthcare by predicting disease outbreaks.
