# Module 4 Assignment: Ethical AI for Community Impact

## **Assignment Title:**  
**Building a Fairness-Aware AI Model for Community Service Decision-Making**

---

## **1. Objectives**  
By completing this assignment, students will:  
- Design and implement a fairness-aware machine learning model.  
- Apply ethical AI principles to real-world decision-making scenarios.  
- Learn how AI can positively impact communities through equitable solutions.  
- Reflect on the kingdom perspective of using AI for justice and service.  

---

## **2. Detailed Instructions**  

### **Project Scenario**  
You are part of a team tasked with developing an AI system to assist a local nonprofit organization in allocating resources (e.g., food, housing aid, educational programs) to underserved communities. The organization wants to ensure that resource allocation is fair and unbiased, avoiding any potential discrimination based on demographic factors such as race, gender, or socioeconomic status.  

Your goal is to build a machine learning model that predicts the need for resources while incorporating fairness constraints to minimize bias.  

### **Steps to Complete the Assignment**  
1. **Data Collection & Exploration**  
   - Use a publicly available dataset (e.g., U.S. Census data, Kaggle datasets on poverty or community needs) or create a synthetic dataset that includes features such as income level, education, family size, and demographic information.  
   - Explore the dataset to identify potential biases or imbalances.  

2. **Model Development**  
   - Train a machine learning model (e.g., logistic regression, decision tree, or neural network) to predict resource need based on the dataset.  
   - Incorporate fairness metrics (e.g., demographic parity, equalized odds) using libraries like `AI Fairness 360` or `Fairlearn`.  
   - Evaluate the model’s performance using both accuracy and fairness metrics.  

3. **Ethical Reflection**  
   - Write a short reflection (300-500 words) on how your model aligns with kingdom values (e.g., justice, compassion, stewardship).  
   - Discuss potential societal impacts of deploying this model in a real-world setting.  

4. **Presentation**  
   - Prepare a 5-minute presentation summarizing your model, its fairness improvements, and its potential community impact.  

---

## **3. Real-World Application**  
This project simulates a real-world scenario where AI is used to make decisions that directly affect people’s lives. By focusing on fairness, students will learn how to develop AI systems that promote equity and justice, aligning with the mission of serving underserved communities.
