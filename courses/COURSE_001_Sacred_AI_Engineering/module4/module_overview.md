```markdown
# Module 4: Ethical Foundations & Neural Networks in Sacred AI Engineering  

**Course:** Sacred AI & Machine Learning Engineering  
**Level:** Undergraduate  
**Category:** Technology  

---

## Learning Objectives  
By the end of this module, students will be able to:  
1. **Explain** the biblical framework for ethical AI development and contrast it with secular approaches.  
2. **Design** a simple neural network using Python, demonstrating understanding of layers, activation functions, and backpropagation.  
3. **Evaluate** real-world AI applications through the lens of Proverbs-based wisdom (e.g., fairness, transparency).  
4. **Propose** guardrails for AI systems that align with Micah 6:8’s call for justice, mercy, and humility.  

---

## Key Concepts Covered  
- **Theology of Technology:** Imago Dei (Genesis 1:27) and humanity’s role as stewards of innovation.  
- **Neural Network Fundamentals:** Perceptrons, hidden layers, gradient descent, and overfitting.  
- **Ethical AI:** Bias mitigation, explainability, and the "double love command" (Matthew 22:37-39) in algorithm design.  
- **Sacred Debugging:** Spiritual disciplines (e.g., prayerful reflection) applied to troubleshooting ML models.  

---

## Scripture Foundations  
1. **Proverbs 2:6** – "For the Lord gives wisdom; from his mouth come knowledge and understanding." *(Basis for seeking divine insight in technical work)*  
2. **Romans 12:2** – "Do not conform to the pattern of this world but be transformed by the renewing of your mind." *(Call to rethink AI ethics beyond cultural norms)*  

---

## Real-World Applications  
- **Bias Auditing:** Analyzing facial recognition systems for racial/gender disparities.  
- **Church Analytics:** Using neural networks to optimize community outreach while preserving privacy.  
- **Prophecy & Pattern Recognition:** Exploring how ML can identify biblical typology (e.g., Christological patterns in Old Testament narratives).  

---

## Module Structure (4 Lectures)  
1. **Lecture 4.1:** *From Babel to Backpropagation* – Biblical stewardship of complex systems.  
   - Activity: Journaling reflection on Genesis 11 and collaborative AI risks.  
2. **Lecture 4.2:** *Neural Networks as Parables* – How layers mimic spiritual growth (1 Corinthians 3:2 → "milk to meat").  
   - Lab: Building a MNIST classifier with Keras.  
3. **Lecture 4.3:** *Righteous Algorithms* – Case studies on AI ethics in hiring and criminal justice.  
   - Debate: "Can an AI system exhibit agape love?"  
4. **Lecture 4.4:** *Sanctified Models* – Regularization techniques and spiritual disciplines (fasting from data to avoid idolatry).  
   - Project: Ethical review of an existing AI product.  

---

## Assessment Overview  
- **Devotional Code Review (30%):** Annotate a neural network script with Scripture references explaining design choices.  
- **Ethical Simulation (20%):** Role-play as a Christian AI engineer advising a startup on bias mitigation.  
- **Final Project (50%):** Train a model to solve a community problem (e.g., poverty prediction) with a 500-word "theology of optimization" statement.  

---

## Estimated Time Commitment  
- **Lectures & Labs:** 12 hours  
- **Scripture Integration Exercises:** 3 hours  
- **Assessments:** 10 hours  
- **Total:** 25 hours  

*"Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord" (Colossians 3:23) – including debugging!*  
```  

### Scroll Pedagogy Highlights:  
- **Revelation + Reason:** Scripture frames technical concepts (e.g., backpropagation as "bearing one another’s burdens").  
- **Transformation:** Assessments require applying faith to engineering decisions.  
- **Ascension:** Progresses from theory (lectures) to ethical action (simulation/project).  
- **Theory-Practice Tie:** Labs use real datasets while devotional components root work in worship.
