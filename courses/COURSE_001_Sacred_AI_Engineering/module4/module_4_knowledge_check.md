## **Multiple Choice Questions**  

1. **Which of the following best describes supervised learning?**  
   a) Learning from unlabeled data  
   b) Learning from labeled data with input-output pairs  
   c) Learning by interacting with an environment  
   d) Learning without any data  

   **Answer:** b) Learning from labeled data with input-output pairs  
   **Explanation:** Supervised learning involves training a model using labeled data, where the correct output for each input is provided. This aligns with the concept of teaching with clear guidance, akin to Proverbs 22:6, which emphasizes training in the right way.  

2. **What is the primary purpose of a validation set in machine learning?**  
   a) To train the model  
   b) To test the model on unseen data  
   c) To tune hyperparameters and prevent overfitting  
   d) To label the data  

   **Answer:** c) To tune hyperparameters and prevent overfitting  
   **Explanation:** The validation set helps evaluate the model's performance during training and ensures it generalizes well to new data. This mirrors the biblical principle of testing and refining, as in Proverbs 27:17, "As iron sharpens iron."  

3. **Which activation function is most commonly used in hidden layers of neural networks?**  
   a) Sigmoid  
   b) Softmax  
   c) ReLU  
   d) Tanh  

   **Answer:** c) ReLU  
   **Explanation:** ReLU (Rectified Linear Unit) is widely used because it introduces non-linearity while avoiding the vanishing gradient problem. This reflects the balance between simplicity and effectiveness, much like the wisdom of Ecclesiastes 7:16, "Do not be overly righteous."  

4. **What is the primary ethical concern when deploying AI systems?**  
   a) Computational efficiency  
   b) Bias and fairness  
   c) Model accuracy  
   d) Data storage  

   **Answer:** b) Bias and fairness  
   **Explanation:** Ensuring fairness and avoiding bias is crucial to uphold justice, as emphasized in Micah 6:8, "Act justly and love mercy."  

5. **Which biblical principle aligns with the concept of transparency in AI development?**  
   a) "The truth will set you free" (John 8:32)  
   b) "Love your neighbor as yourself" (Mark 12:31)  
   c) "Do not judge by appearances" (John 7:24)  
   d) "Do not be anxious about anything" (Philippians 4:6)  

   **Answer:** a) "The truth will set you free" (John 8:32)  
   **Explanation:** Transparency in AI ensures that decisions are explainable and truthful, reflecting the biblical value of honesty.  

6. **What does the term "overfitting" mean in machine learning?**  
   a) The model performs well on training data but poorly on unseen data  
   b) The model performs poorly on both training and unseen data  
   c) The model performs well on unseen data but poorly on training data  
   d) The model performs equally well on training and unseen data  

   **Answer:** a) The model performs well on training data but poorly on unseen data  
   **Explanation:** Overfitting occurs when a model learns the training data too well, capturing noise rather than general patterns. This can be likened to focusing on appearances rather than substance, as warned in 1 Samuel 16:7.  

7. **Which of the following is an example of unsupervised learning?**  
   a) Predicting house prices  
   b) Classifying emails as spam or not spam  
   c) Grouping customers based on purchasing behavior  
   d) Recognizing handwritten digits  

   **Answer:** c) Grouping customers based on purchasing behavior  
   **Explanation:** Unsupervised learning involves finding patterns in unlabeled data, such as clustering. This reflects the idea of discovering hidden truths, as in Proverbs 25:2, "It is the glory of God to conceal a matter."  

8. **What is the primary purpose of regularization in machine learning?**  
   a) To increase model complexity  
   b) To reduce overfitting  
   c) To speed up training  
   d) To improve accuracy on training data  

   **Answer:** b) To reduce overfitting  
   **Explanation:** Regularization techniques like L1/L2 regularization prevent models from becoming too complex, promoting generalization. This aligns with the biblical principle of moderation, as in Philippians 4:5, "Let your moderation be known to all."  

9. **Which biblical story illustrates the importance of wisdom in decision-making, akin to AI's role in assisting human choices?**  
   a) David and Goliath  
   b) Solomon’s judgment  
   c) Noah’s Ark  
   d) The Prodigal Son  

   **Answer:** b) Solomon’s judgment  
   **Explanation:** Solomon’s wisdom in discerning the true mother of the child (1 Kings 3:16-28) exemplifies the value of wise decision-making, similar to AI’s role in aiding human judgments.  

10. **What is the primary goal of ethical AI development?**  
    a) Maximizing profit  
    b) Ensuring fairness, accountability, and transparency  
    c) Minimizing computational costs  
    d) Increasing model complexity  

    **Answer:** b) Ensuring fairness, accountability, and transparency  
    **Explanation:** Ethical AI prioritizes justice, accountability, and transparency, reflecting biblical values such as Micah 6:8 and Proverbs 11:1.

2. **Bias in AI systems is always intentional.**  
   **Answer:** False  
   **Reasoning:** Bias can arise unintentionally from biased data or flawed algorithms. Proverbs 18:17 reminds us to seek all sides of a matter before judging.  

3. **AI can replace the need for human creativity and ingenuity.**  
   **Answer:** False  
   **Reasoning:** Creativity is a gift from God (Exodus 31:3), and AI serves as a tool to enhance, not replace, human creativity.  

4. **The development of AI should prioritize efficiency over ethical considerations.**  
   **Answer:** False  
   **Reasoning:** Ethical considerations must take precedence, as Jesus taught in Mark 8:36, "What good is it to gain the whole world yet forfeit your soul?"  

5. **AI can fully understand and replicate human emotions.**  
   **Answer:** False  
   **Reasoning:** Emotions are deeply tied to the human soul, created in God’s image (Psalm 139:14). AI can simulate but not replicate this depth.
