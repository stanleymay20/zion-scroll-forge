# **Module 7 Assignment: Ethical AI for Social Impact**  
**Course:** Sacred AI & Machine Learning Engineering  
**Level:** Undergraduate  
**Estimated Time:** 5-10 hours  

---

## **1. Assignment Objectives**  
By completing this assignment, you will:  
- Apply machine learning techniques to solve a real-world problem with ethical considerations.  
- Develop a practical AI solution that aligns with kingdom values (justice, compassion, stewardship).  
- Demonstrate proficiency in data preprocessing, model training, and evaluation.  
- Reflect on the societal and spiritual implications of your AI solution.  

---

## **2. Project Instructions**  
### **Problem Statement**  
Choose **one** of the following real-world scenarios where AI can make a positive impact:  
1. **Poverty Alleviation:** Predict areas most in need of food aid using socioeconomic data.  
2. **Mental Health Support:** Build a chatbot that provides biblical encouragement and mental health resources.  
3. **Environmental Stewardship:** Analyze satellite images to detect deforestation or pollution hotspots.  

### **Tasks**  
#### **A. Data & Preprocessing (2-3 hours)**  
- Identify and collect a dataset (e.g., Kaggle, government open data, or synthetic data).  
- Clean and preprocess the data (handle missing values, normalize features, etc.).  
- Justify your preprocessing steps from an ethical perspective (e.g., bias mitigation).  

#### **B. Model Development (3-4 hours)**  
- Train a machine learning model (e.g., linear regression, decision tree, or simple neural network).  
- Evaluate performance using appropriate metrics (accuracy, F1-score, MSE, etc.).  
- Document limitations (e.g., bias in data, computational constraints).  

#### **C. Kingdom Impact Reflection (1-2 hours)**  
- Write a 300-word reflection on:  
  - How your solution aligns with biblical principles (e.g., loving neighbors, caring for creation).  
  - Potential risks or ethical concerns (e.g., privacy, misuse).  
  - How this project could be used in ministry or vocational missions.  

---

## **3. Deliverables**  
Submit the following in a **single ZIP file**:  
1. **Code:** Well-commented Jupyter Notebook or Python script.  
2. **Report (PDF):**  
   - Problem statement and dataset description.  
   - Methodology (preprocessing, model choice, evaluation).  
   - Kingdom impact reflection.  
3. **Presentation (3-5 slides or 2-min video):**  
   - Summarize your project for a non-technical audience (e.g., church leaders).
