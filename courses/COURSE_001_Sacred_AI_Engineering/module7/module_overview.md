# Module 7: Ethical AI Development & Kingdom Impact

### Module Number: 7
**Module Title:** Ethical AI Development & Kingdom Impact  
**Course:** Sacred AI & Machine Learning Engineering  
**Level:** Undergraduate  
**Category:** Technology  

---

## Learning Objectives
By the end of this module, students will be able to:  
1. **Analyze** ethical dilemmas in AI development from a kingdom perspective.  
2. **Design** AI systems that align with biblical principles of justice, stewardship, and love.  
3. **Evaluate** the societal impact of AI technologies on marginalized communities.  
4. **Apply** ethical frameworks to real-world AI engineering scenarios.  
5. **Reflect** on the role of AI in advancing God’s kingdom on earth.  

---

## Key Concepts Covered
- Ethical frameworks in AI (utilitarianism, deontology, virtue ethics).  
- Bias and fairness in machine learning models.  
- Stewardship of technology and resources.  
- AI for social good and kingdom impact.  
- Transparency, accountability, and trust in AI systems.  

---

## Scripture Foundations
1. **Micah 6:8** - "He has shown you, O mortal, what is good. And what does the Lord require of you? To act justly and to love mercy and to walk humbly with your God."  
2. **Proverbs 3:27** - "Do not withhold good from those to whom it is due, when it is in your power to act."  
3. **Colossians 3:23-24** - "Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord, not for human masters."  

---

## Real-World Applications
- Developing AI systems to reduce bias in hiring practices.  
- Designing algorithms to improve access to healthcare in underserved communities.  
- Creating transparent AI tools for government decision-making.  
- Leveraging AI to combat human trafficking and other injustices.  

---

## Module Structure
This module consists of four lectures, each integrating revelation and reason for transformative learning:  

### Lecture 1: Foundations of Ethical AI  
- Overview of ethical theories.  
- Biblical principles for ethical technology development.  
- Case study: Bias in facial recognition systems.  

### Lecture 2: AI for Social Good  
- Exploring AI applications that serve marginalized communities.  
- Scripture reflection: Stewardship and justice.  
- Group discussion: Identifying kingdom opportunities in AI.  

### Lecture 3: Transparency & Accountability in AI  
- The importance of explainability in AI systems.  
- Biblical examples of accountability.  
- Hands-on activity: Designing a transparent AI model.  

### Lecture 4: AI & Kingdom Impact  
- Vision for AI as a tool for advancing God’s kingdom.  
- Case study: AI in humanitarian efforts.  
- Final reflection: Personal calling in AI engineering.  

---

## Assessment Overview
1. **Ethical AI Case Study Analysis (30%)** - Students will analyze a real-world AI case study and propose solutions grounded in ethical and biblical principles.  
2. **Group Project: AI for Social Good (40%)** - Teams will design an AI system addressing a specific societal issue, presenting their solution and its kingdom impact.  
3. **Reflection Paper (30%)** - A personal reflection on how AI can be used to glorify God and serve humanity.  

---

## Estimated Time Commitment
- **Lectures & Readings:** 8 hours  
- **Case Study Analysis:** 4 hours  
- **Group Project:** 10 hours  
- **Reflection Paper:** 3 hours  
**Total:** 25 hours  

---

This module integrates revelation and reason, focusing on transformation over information, and ties theory to practice, equipping students to engineer AI systems that reflect God’s love and justice.
