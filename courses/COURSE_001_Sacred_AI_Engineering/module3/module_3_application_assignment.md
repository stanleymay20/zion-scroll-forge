# Module 3 Assignment: Ethical AI for Social Good  
**Course:** Sacred AI & Machine Learning Engineering  
**Level:** Undergraduate  
**Estimated Time:** 5-10 hours  

---

## **1. Objectives**  
By completing this assignment, students will:  
- Apply foundational machine learning (ML) techniques to solve a real-world problem.  
- Evaluate ethical implications of AI solutions from a "kingdom perspective" (e.g., fairness, justice, compassion).  
- Design an ML model with measurable societal or ministry impact.  
- Present technical work clearly for non-technical stakeholders.  

---

## **2. Instructions**  
### **Project Overview**  
Develop a **binary classification model** to address a social or ministry challenge (e.g., predicting food insecurity, identifying at-risk youth, optimizing charity resource allocation). Use a provided dataset or source your own (e.g., [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets), [UCI ML Repository](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/ml/index.php)).  

### **Steps**  
1. **Problem Selection** (1 hour):  
   - Choose a problem aligned with Christian values (e.g., poverty alleviation, mental health support).  
   - Justify your choice in 1–2 paragraphs.  

2. **Data Preparation** (2 hours):  
   - Clean, preprocess, and explore the dataset.  
   - Document ethical risks (e.g., bias in data).  

3. **Model Development** (3 hours):  
   - Train a binary classifier (e.g., logistic regression, decision tree).  
   - Evaluate performance (accuracy, precision, recall).  

4. **Kingdom Impact Analysis** (1 hour):  
   - Explain how your model could empower a ministry/NGO or address injustice.  

5. **Submission** (1 hour):  
   - Compile deliverables (see below).  

---

## **3. Deliverables**  
Submit a **single PDF or GitHub repository** containing:  
1. **Code**: Jupyter Notebook or Python script with comments.  
2. **Report** (2–3 pages):  
   - Problem statement and dataset source.  
   - Ethical considerations.  
   - Model performance metrics.  
   - 3–5 bullet points on potential kingdom impact.  
3. **5-Minute Video**: Explain your project to a non-technical audience (e.g., church leaders).  

---

## **4. Rubric**  
| Criteria               | Excellent (4) | Proficient (3) | Developing (2) | Needs Work (1) |  
|------------------------|--------------|---------------|---------------|---------------|  
| **Technical Accuracy** | Flawless model implementation | Minor errors, fully functional | Major errors, partial functionality | Model fails |  
| **Ethical Reflection** | Deep analysis of bias/justice | Identifies key ethical risks | Surface-level discussion | Missing |  
| **Kingdom Impact**     | Clear, actionable ministry plan | General societal benefit mentioned | Vague connection | No connection |  
| **Clarity**           | Professional report/video | Minor clarity issues | Disorganized | Unsubmittable |  

---

## **5. Submission Guidelines**  
- Due: **2 weeks from assignment date**.  
- Submit via LMS (PDF) or GitHub link (ensure repo is public).  
- Late policy: 10% deduction per day (max 2 days late).  

---

## **6. Kingdom Impact Focus**  
Your project should answer:  
- *How can this model reflect God’s heart for justice?*  
- *Who in your community could benefit from this tool?*  

**Example Projects:**  
- Predict homelessness risk to prioritize shelter outreach.  
- Classify toxic social media comments for a Christian counseling service.  
- Optimize donation matching for a food bank.  

---
