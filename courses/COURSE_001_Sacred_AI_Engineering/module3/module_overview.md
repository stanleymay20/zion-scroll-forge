# **Module 3: Neural Networks & Divine Design**  
**Course:** Sacred AI & Machine Learning Engineering  
**Level:** Undergraduate  
**Category:** Technology  

---

## **1. Module Title & Number**  
**Title:** *Neural Networks & Divine Design: Architecting Intelligence with Wisdom*  
**Module Number:** 3  

---

## **2. Learning Objectives**  
By the end of this module, students will be able to:  
1. **Explain** the biological inspiration behind artificial neural networks (ANNs) and their theological parallels (e.g., "fearfully and wonderfully made").  
2. **Design** a basic feedforward neural network using Python frameworks (TensorFlow/PyTorch).  
3. **Evaluate** ethical implications of neural networks in decision-making (e.g., bias, accountability).  
4. **Apply** neural networks to solve a real-world problem aligned with kingdom values (e.g., poverty prediction, ethical automation).  
5. **Reflect** on how ANNs mirror God’s creative patterns in nature (e.g., complexity, adaptability).  

---

## **3. Key Concepts Covered**  
- Biological vs. Artificial Neurons  
- Perceptrons and Activation Functions  
- Backpropagation & Gradient Descent  
- Overfitting & Regularization (with wisdom principles)  
- Ethical AI: Bias, Explainability, and Stewardship

## **5. Real-World Applications**  
- **Medical Diagnostics:** Neural networks for early disease detection (e.g., cancer screening).  
- **Ethical Automation:** AI-driven poverty mapping for humanitarian aid.  
- **Creative Stewardship:** Generative AI for worship music/art with intentionality.  

---

## **6. Module Structure (4 Lectures)**  
**Lecture 1:** *From Neurons to Networks*  
- Biological inspiration, perceptrons, and theological parallels.  
- **Activity:** Compare brain plasticity vs. ANN adaptability.  

**Lecture 2:** *Training & Transformation*  
- Backpropagation, loss functions, and spiritual growth analogies.  
- **Lab:** Build a single-layer NN for binary classification.  

**Lecture 3:** *Ethics in the Hidden Layers*  
- Bias in datasets, model interpretability, and biblical justice.  
- **Case Study:** Analyze a biased AI system and propose redemptive solutions.  

**Lecture 4:** *Sacred Applications*  
- Neural networks for societal good (e.g., disaster response, ethical fintech).  
- **Project Pitch:** Propose a kingdom-focused ANN application.  

---

## **7. Assessment Overview**  
- **Quiz (20%):** MCQs on ANN fundamentals and ethical considerations.  
- **Lab Report (30%):** Document a neural network implementation with theological reflections.  
- **Ethics Debate (20%):** Group discussion on AI accountability.  
- **Final Project (30%):** Design an ANN prototype for a real-world problem + 500-word kingdom impact analysis.
