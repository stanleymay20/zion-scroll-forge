```markdown
# Module 1: Foundations of Global Trade & Biblical Justice  

**Course:** Global Trade & Kingdom Commerce  
**Level:** Undergraduate  
**Category:** Economics  

---

## **Learning Objectives**  
By the end of this module, students will be able to:  
1. **Define** the core principles of international trade and contrast them with biblical justice frameworks.  
2. **Analyze** the ethical implications of trade policies using scriptural principles (e.g., fairness, stewardship).  
3. **Evaluate** case studies of global commerce through the lens of "Kingdom Economics."  
4. **Propose** practical ways to integrate justice into trade practices at individual and organizational levels.  

---

## **Key Concepts Covered**  
- **Biblical Foundations of Commerce:** Stewardship, justice, and equity in trade.  
- **Comparative Advantage vs. Ethical Responsibility:** Balancing efficiency with moral imperatives.  
- **Trade Policy & Justice:** Tariffs, subsidies, and their impact on vulnerable populations.  
- **Kingdom Economics:** Aligning profit motives with divine purpose (e.g., "Seek first the Kingdom" – Matt 6:33).  

---

## **Scripture Foundations**  
1. **Proverbs 11:1** – *"Dishonest scales are an abomination to the Lord, but a just weight is His delight."*  
   - *Application:* Integrity in trade measurements and contracts.  
2. **Leviticus 19:35-36** – *"Do not use dishonest standards when measuring length, weight, or quantity."*  
   - *Application:* Ethical accountability in global supply chains.  
3. **Micah 6:8** – *"What does the Lord require? To act justly, love mercy, and walk humbly with your God."*  
   - *Application:* Justice as a non-negotiable in trade decisions.  

---

## **Real-World Applications**  
- **Case Study:** Fair Trade vs. Free Trade – Examining coffee supply chains for wage equity.  
- **Policy Debate:** Should nations prioritize protectionism to safeguard local labor?  
- **Personal Reflection:** How can individual consumption habits reflect biblical justice?  

---

## **Module Structure (4 Lectures)**  
1. **Lecture 1:** *Biblical Frameworks for Trade*  
   - Revelation: Scripture’s view of commerce.  
   - Reason: Economic theories vs. Kingdom values.  
2. **Lecture 2:** *Ethics of Trade Policy*  
   - Progressive Ascension: From theory (comparative advantage) to justice (fair wages).  
3. **Lecture 3:** *Global Supply Chains & Justice*  
   - Transformation Focus: How students can advocate for ethical sourcing.  
4. **Lecture 4:** *Kingdom Commerce in Practice*  
   - Tie to Practice: Guest speaker (Christian entrepreneur) on integrating faith and trade.  

---

## **Assessment Overview**  
1. **Reflection Journal (20%)** – Personal responses to scripture and trade ethics.  
2. **Case Study Analysis (30%)** – Evaluate a trade policy using biblical principles.  
3. **Group Debate (25%)** – Argue for/against a trade policy with justice considerations.  
4. **Action Plan (25%)** – Design a "Kingdom Commerce" initiative for a local business.  

---

## **Estimated Time Commitment**  
- **Lectures & Readings:** 6 hours  
- **Assignments & Group Work:** 8 hours  
- **Personal Reflection & Study:** 4 hours  
- **Total:** 18 hours  

*"True commerce glorifies God when it uplifts the marginalized."*  
```  

### **Pedagogical Notes**  
- **Scroll Model Alignment:**  
  - *Revelation + Reason:* Scripture and economic theory intertwined.  
  - *Transformation:* Assessments focus on actionable change, not just knowledge.  
  - *Progressive Ascension:* Builds from theory (Lecture 1) to real-world advocacy (Lecture 4).  
  - *Theory to Practice:* Case studies and guest speaker bridge gaps.
