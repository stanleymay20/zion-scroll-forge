```markdown
# Module 1: Foundations of Digital Currency & Kingdom Economics  
**Course:** ScrollCoin & Digital Currency Systems  
**Level:** Undergraduate | **Category:** Economics  

---

## **Learning Objectives**  
By the end of this module, students will be able to:  
1. Define cryptocurrency, blockchain, and digital economics using biblical principles of stewardship.  
2. Analyze the role of trust in financial systems by contrasting centralized (traditional) and decentralized (blockchain) models.  
3. Evaluate the ethical implications of digital currencies through the lens of Scripture (e.g., honesty, fairness, and accountability).  
4. Demonstrate how blockchain technology aligns with or challenges kingdom values like transparency and decentralization.  
5. Propose practical ways to use digital currencies for missional or community-building purposes.  

---

## **Key Concepts Covered**  
- **Biblical Stewardship & Economics:** God’s design for resource management (e.g., parables of talents/minas).  
- **Trust & Decentralization:** How blockchain replaces intermediaries with cryptographic verification.  
- **Money as a Tool:** Contrasting secular and kingdom perspectives on wealth (1 Timothy 6:10 vs. Matthew 6:21).  
- **Transparency & Accountability:** Blockchain’s immutable ledger and its parallels to Proverbs 11:1.  

---

## **Scripture Foundations**  
1. **Proverbs 3:9-10** – Honoring God with wealth and "firstfruits" in digital economies.  
2. **Luke 16:10-11** – Faithfulness in handling money (applied to crypto transactions).  
3. **Acts 2:44-45** – Early church resource-sharing as a model for decentralized finance (DeFi).  

---

## **Real-World Applications**  
- Case Study: How churches/nonprofits accept crypto donations.  
- Debate: Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs) vs. kingdom values of financial freedom.  
- Practical Exercise: Setting up a non-custodial wallet to practice self-sovereignty.  

---

## **Module Structure (4 Lectures)**  
1. **Lecture 1:** *Biblical Economics Meets Digital Money*  
   - Stewardship in the digital age.  
2. **Lecture 2:** *Trust Machines: Blockchain as a Kingdom Tool?*  
   - How consensus algorithms reflect (or distort) biblical community.  
3. **Lecture 3:** *Ethics of Crypto: Greed, Generosity, and Governance*  
   - Analyzing ICOs, scams, and ethical investing.  
4. **Lecture 4:** *Hands-On Faith: Using Crypto for Mission*  
   - Demo: Sending ScrollCoin to a ministry partner.  

---

## **Assessment Overview**  
- **Reflection Journal (20%):** Personal response to Scripture and crypto ethics.  
- **Group Debate (30%):** "Resolved: Decentralization aligns with biblical community."  
- **Practical Project (50%):** Design a kingdom-centered use case for blockchain (e.g., transparent charity ledger).  

---

## **Estimated Time Commitment**  
- **Lectures & Readings:** 6 hours  
- **Assignments & Group Work:** 8 hours  
- **Practical Exercises:** 4 hours  
- **Total:** 18 hours  

> *"The earth is the Lord’s, and everything in it" (Psalm 24:1). This module challenges students to reimagine finance as a tool for God’s purposes.*  
``` 

### **Pedagogy Notes**  
- **Revelation + Reason:** Scripture frames technical discussions (e.g., blockchain immutability vs. God’s unchanging nature).  
- **Transformation:** Focus shifts from "how crypto works" to "how crypto serves."  
- **Progressive Ascension:** Starts with theory (Lecture 1), ends with action (Lecture 4).  
- **Theory to Practice:** Assessments require real-world application (e.g., designing a ministry wallet).
