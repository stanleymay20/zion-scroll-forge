**Module 3: The ScrollUniversity Ethos: Mission, Vision, and Educational Philosophy**
**Course: Foundations of ScrollUniversity**

---

### 1. Description

This module immerses students in the foundational identity of ScrollUniversity by exploring its core ethos as articulated in its Mission, Vision, and Educational Philosophy. We will dissect the official statements to understand not just what they say, but the profound theological and pedagogical convictions that underpin them. Students will learn that our mission to "equip servant-leaders for transformative impact through biblically grounded, academically excellent education" is more than a slogan; it is a covenant commitment to forming whole persons for a life of faithful service in the Kingdom of God.

The module will then transition to a detailed examination of our Christ-centered educational philosophy, which integrates faith, learning, and practice. We will explore how this philosophy rejects the modern secular/sacred divide and instead posits that all truth is God's truth, making every academic discipline a potential avenue for divine revelation and worship. Students will analyze how this holistic approach shapes our curriculum design, classroom instruction, faculty hiring, and community life, ensuring that every aspect of the ScrollUniversity experience is intentionally formative and aligned with our ultimate purpose.

### 2. Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this module, you will be able to:

*   Articulate the core components of the ScrollUniversity Mission and Vision statements in your own words.
*   Explain the theological and philosophical foundations that undergird the ScrollUniversity educational model.
*   Analyze how the university's ethos directly influences its curricular and co-curricular programs.
*   Evaluate personal educational and spiritual goals in light of the ScrollUniversity mission.
*   Formulate a preliminary understanding of how your chosen field of study can be integrated with your Christian faith within the ScrollUniversity context.
*   Identify specific practices and resources at ScrollUniversity designed to support your spiritual and academic formation.

### 3. Spiritual Formation Focus

The spiritual formation focus of this module is **Intentional Discipleship and Vocational Clarity**. The process of understanding and internalizing the university's ethos is, at its heart, a spiritual exercise. It calls you to reflect on your identity in Christ and how your God-given gifts, passions, and academic pursuits align with His purposes. By engaging with our mission, you are invited to see your education not as a mere transaction for a degree, but as a sacred journey of being shaped into the person God has created you to be. This module will challenge you to move beyond passive learning to active participation in a community dedicated to following Jesus, loving God with all your mind, and serving your neighbor through your future vocation.

### 4. Connection to Course

This module serves as the theological and philosophical heart of the "Foundations of ScrollUniversity" course. While previous modules established the historical context of Christian higher education and the biblical basis for learning, this module makes that foundation concrete and specific to your experience at ScrollUniversity. It answers the critical "So what?" question by defining the unique character of the community you have joined. The principles explored here will provide the essential lens through which you will interpret and engage with all subsequent courses, relationships, and opportunities at the university, ensuring your entire educational journey is coherent, purposeful, and deeply rooted in our shared Christian commitment.
