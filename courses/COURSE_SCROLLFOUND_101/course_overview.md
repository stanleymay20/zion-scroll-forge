# Foundations of ScrollUniversity

## Course Information
- **Code**: SCROLLFOUND_101
- **Credits**: 3
- **Level**: Foundation
- **Modules**: 4
- **Total Lectures**: 12

**COURSE OVERVIEW: SCROLLFOUND_101 - FOUNDATIONS OF SCROLLUNIVERSITY**

**1. Detailed Course Description**

Foundations of ScrollUniversity is the cornerstone course designed to immerse students in the unique educational philosophy and spiritual mission of our institution. This 3-credit foundation course establishes the framework for Kingdom Education, moving beyond mere information transfer to transformational learning that integrates faith, reason, and practice. Students will explore what it means to be educated not just for career success but for eternal impact, understanding how their academic journey connects to God's redemptive work in the world. The course challenges the secular/sacred divide in education and presents learning as an act of worship and stewardship.

Through intensive study of biblical worldview foundations and personal calling discovery, students will develop a robust theological understanding of education's purpose from creation to new creation. The curriculum examines how the Fall has distorted educational paradigms and how Christ's redemption restores learning to its proper place in God's Kingdom. Students will engage with historical models of Christian education while developing practical tools for implementing Kingdom principles in their current studies and future vocations. The course emphasizes that all truth is God's truth and that every academic discipline finds its ultimate meaning in Christ.

The culminating experience involves students creating a personalized Educational Mandate that integrates their spiritual gifts, passions, academic interests, and Kingdom priorities. This document serves as a living guide for their educational journey at ScrollUniversity and beyond, ensuring their learning remains purpose-driven and eternally significant. Through this process, students transition from being passive recipients of education to active stewards of their God-given intellectual and spiritual capacities.

**2. Learning Objectives**
Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:
- Articulate a comprehensive biblical theology of education that encompasses creation, fall, redemption, and restoration
- Analyze and critique secular educational paradigms through the lens of Kingdom priorities and values
- Identify their primary spiritual gifts, natural abilities, and God-given passions through validated assessment tools
- Develop a personalized calling statement that integrates faith, learning, and vocational direction
- Apply principles of Kingdom stewardship to their current academic work and study habits
- Create a four-year educational plan that aligns course selection with calling development
- Demonstrate understanding of how their chosen major connects to God's redemptive work in the world
- Formulate a practical strategy for maintaining spiritual formation while pursuing academic excellence
- Defend the value of Christian liberal arts education in preparing for diverse vocational contexts
- Produce a comprehensive Educational Mandate document to guide their ongoing development

**3. Spiritual Formation Integration**

Spiritual formation is woven throughout the course structure through multiple integrated practices. Students maintain a weekly Spiritual Learning Journal where they document how course concepts intersect with their personal walk with Christ. Each class session begins with liturgical learning prayers specifically designed for academic contexts, reinforcing that all learning is spiritual activity. Students participate in triads for weekly accountability focused on integrating learning with spiritual practices.

The curriculum includes specific formation exercises such as the "Vocation Examen" - a guided reflection on where students have experienced God's presence in their academic work and career aspirations. Students practice "Lectio Divina" with biblical texts about wisdom, knowledge, and understanding to develop a prayerful approach to learning. The course incorporates regular fasting from digital distractions to create space for deep reflection and hearing God's voice regarding educational direction.

**4. Real-World Deployment Pathways**

Students immediately apply course concepts through three primary deployment pathways:
- **Academic Integration Project**: Students audit one of their current courses through a Kingdom education lens, producing a 5-page analysis of how the subject matter connects to biblical themes and Kingdom priorities
- **Vocational Shadowing**: Each student completes 8 hours of shadowing a professional in their field of interest, focusing on observing how faith integrates with vocational practice
- **Community Learning Initiative**: Students design and implement a small educational project in their local community (church, neighborhood, workplace) that applies Kingdom education principles to teach others

**5. Assessment Strategy**

Student learning is evaluated through multiple authentic assessment methods:
- **Educational Mandate Portfolio (40%)**: A comprehensive document including spiritual gifts assessment, calling statement, four-year academic plan, and vocational integration strategy
- **Worldview Analysis Paper (25%)**: A 10-12 page critical analysis comparing secular and Christian educational paradigms with robust biblical and theological support
- **Learning Integration Projects (20%)**: Practical applications including the Academic Integration Project and Community Learning Initiative with reflection components
- **Class Participation and Spiritual Journals (15%)**: Ongoing engagement with course materials, triad participation, and documented spiritual formation journey

**6. Prerequisites and Required Materials**

**Prerequisites**: None. This is a foundation course required for all incoming students.

**Required Texts**:
- Holmes, Arthur F. "The Idea of a Christian College" (Revised Edition). Eerdmans, 1987.
- Parrett, Gary A. and S. Steve Kang. "Teaching the Faith, Forming the Faithful: A Biblical Vision for Education in the Church." IVP Academic, 2009.
- Veith, Gene Edward. "God at Work: Your Christian Vocation in All of Life." Crossway, 2002.
- ScrollUniversity Custom Reader: "Kingdom Education: Foundational Readings" (provided digitally)

**Additional Materials**:
- Spiritual Gifts Assessment (provided through campus ministry office)
- StrengthsFinder 2.0 by Tom Rath (or access to online assessment)
- ESV Study Bible or equivalent study Bible
- Digital portfolio platform access (provided by university)

**Course Format**: The course meets twice weekly for 75-minute sessions combining lecture, small group discussion, spiritual practices, and workshop-style application sessions. The course is also available in an intensive weekend format for non-traditional students.
