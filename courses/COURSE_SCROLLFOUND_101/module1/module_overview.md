**Module 1: Welcome to ScrollUniversity: Our Mission, Vision, and Educational Philosophy**
**Course: Foundations of ScrollUniversity**

---

### 1. Description

Welcome to the foundational gateway of your journey at ScrollUniversity. This module is designed to immerse you in the very heart of our institution, introducing you to the core principles that define who we are and how we approach the sacred task of Christian education. We will explore our mission to equip disciples of Jesus Christ with wisdom, knowledge, and character for faithful service in the world, and our vision to be a global beacon of transformative biblical truth. You will understand that our educational model is not merely about information transfer but about the holistic formation of the whole person—mind, heart, and hands—rooted in a deep, abiding relationship with God.

This introduction establishes the "why" behind everything you will experience at ScrollUniversity. We will delve into our educational philosophy, which integrates rigorous academic study with intentional spiritual formation, all under the authority of Scripture. You will learn how our commitment to a Christ-centered worldview shapes every course, discussion, and assignment. By the end of this module, you will not just know about ScrollUniversity; you will understand its spirit and be prepared to fully and purposefully engage in the learning community, aligning your personal educational goals with our collective mission to advance the Kingdom of God.

---

### 2. Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this module, you will be able to:

*   Articulate the core components of ScrollUniversity's mission and vision statements in your own words.
*   Explain the key tenets of the ScrollUniversity educational philosophy and how it differs from a secular educational model.
*   Identify the biblical and theological foundations that undergird the university's mission and educational approach.
*   Describe the relationship between academic excellence, spiritual formation, and practical service within the ScrollUniversity model.
*   Reflect on how your personal calling and educational journey align with the mission and values of ScrollUniversity.
*   Begin to formulate a personal learning covenant that incorporates the principles introduced in this module.

---

### 3. Spiritual Formation Focus

The spiritual formation focus for this module is **Intentional Discipleship and Community Integration**. The primary spiritual practice is **prayerful reflection and commitment**. As you learn about the mission and vision of ScrollUniversity, you are encouraged to engage in personal reflection before God, asking how He is calling you to participate in this specific community of learners. The goal is to move beyond being a passive student to becoming an active, invested disciple within our spiritual and academic ecosystem, understanding that your growth is intrinsically linked to the health and purpose of the whole body.

---

### 4. Connection to Course

This module serves as the essential cornerstone for the entire "Foundations of ScrollUniversity" course. It provides the overarching framework, defining the "DNA" of the institution that every subsequent module will build upon. The principles of mission, vision, and philosophy introduced here are the lenses through which you will explore topics like biblical hermeneutics, theological foundations, and Christian worldview in the modules to come. Understanding *who we are* and *why we teach* is the prerequisite for effectively engaging with *what we teach* throughout your studies at ScrollUniversity.
