**Module 2: Stewarding Resources: God's Design for Business and Entrepreneurship**

**1. Description**

This module establishes a foundational biblical theology of stewardship as the core operating principle for Kingdom-oriented business and entrepreneurship. We will explore how Scripture redefines the very concept of "ownership," revealing that God is the ultimate owner of all things and humanity is appointed as His stewards or managers. This paradigm shift moves business from a self-centered pursuit of profit to a God-centered mission of faithful resource management. We will examine the resources God entrusts to us—including capital, creativity, relationships, time, and the earth itself—and our responsibility to cultivate and deploy them for His glory and the flourishing of our communities.

Students will engage with practical case studies and biblical examples, from the Parable of the Talents to the virtuous wife of Proverbs 31, to understand how stewardship principles translate into daily business decisions. This includes ethical sourcing, fair treatment of employees, environmental responsibility, and strategic generosity. The module culminates in developing a framework for viewing a business not as a personal asset but as a ministry of stewardship, where financial sustainability and spiritual obedience are inextricably linked.

**2. Learning Objectives**

Upon successful completion of this module, students will be able to:

*   Articulate a biblical definition of stewardship and contrast it with the world's view of ownership.
*   Identify at least five different types of resources (e.g., financial, intellectual, relational, natural) that God entrusts to business leaders and describe the biblical responsibility associated with each.
*   Analyze a business decision or practice through the lens of faithful stewardship, assessing its alignment with God's design.
*   Develop a personal "Stewardship Manifesto" outlining core principles for managing business resources in a God-honoring way.
*   Create a practical plan for integrating intentional generosity and community impact into a business model.
*   Defend the position that profit, when understood as a resource for stewardship rather than an end in itself, is biblically justified and necessary for sustainable ministry.

**3. Spiritual Formation Focus**

The primary spiritual formation focus of this module is the cultivation of a **Steward's Heart**, characterized by gratitude, trust, and accountability. Through guided reflection and practical application, students will be challenged to confront the idolatry of control and self-reliance that often accompanies entrepreneurship. The goal is to foster a posture of daily dependence on God, recognizing that we are managers of His estate, and to replace anxiety over outcomes with faithfulness in process. Spiritual practices integrated into this module will include the examen of resources, reflective journaling on God's provision, and the discipline of strategic giving.

**4. Connection to Course**

This module on stewardship is the practical outworking of the foundational Kingdom worldview established in Module 1. If Module 1 asked "What is a Kingdom Business?", Module 2 answers the question "How does a Kingdom Business *operate*?" The principles of stewardship directly inform every subsequent module, including leadership (stewarding influence), ethics (stewarding decisions), and marketing (stewarding communication). It provides the essential "how-to" framework for ensuring that a business remains aligned with its ultimate purpose: to glorify God by faithfully managing all He has provided.
