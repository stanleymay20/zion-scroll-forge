**Module 3: The Stewardship Mandate: Managing God's Resources in Business**
**Course: Kingdom Business Principles**

---

### 1. Description

This module establishes the foundational biblical principle that every resource we manage in business—from finances and people to time and influence—is ultimately owned by God and entrusted to us as stewards. We will move beyond the worldly concept of ownership to embrace the Kingdom reality of stewardship, where our primary responsibility is to manage these resources according to God's will and for His purposes. This paradigm shift redefines success, moving it from personal profit and accumulation to faithful management and eternal impact.

Students will explore practical applications of stewardship across key business domains, including financial integrity, ethical labor practices, environmental responsibility, and strategic planning. Through scriptural analysis and case studies, we will dismantle the myth of the "self-made" business leader and replace it with the identity of a faithful servant-manager. This module challenges participants to conduct a thorough audit of their current business practices and develop a concrete, actionable plan to align their resource management with the values of God's Kingdom, ensuring that their enterprise becomes a conduit of His provision and grace.

---

### 2. Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this module, students will be able to:

1.  Articulate a robust biblical theology of stewardship, distinguishing it from secular concepts of ownership.
2.  Identify the key resources (e.g., capital, people, creation, intellectual property) in their business or vocation that fall under the stewardship mandate.
3.  Evaluate their current business practices against biblical standards of financial integrity, justice, and generosity.
4.  Develop and apply ethical frameworks for employee compensation, development, and care that reflect God's heart for justice and dignity.
5.  Formulate a biblically-informed strategy for environmental and social responsibility within their business operations.
6.  Create a personal "Stewardship Declaration" that outlines their commitment to managing God's resources for His glory.

---

### 3. Spiritual Formation Focus

The spiritual formation focus of this module is the cultivation of **Faithful Servanthood**. The primary spiritual discipline practiced will be the **Examination of (Business) Conscience**, where students will regularly bring their business decisions, financial reports, and management strategies before the Lord in prayerful reflection. The goal is to dismantle the strongholds of pride, greed, and anxiety over scarcity, replacing them with the Christ-like virtues of humility, generosity, and trust in God's provision. This formation process aims to transform the participant's identity from "owner" or "boss" to "faithful and wise steward" (Luke 12:42).

---

### 4. Connection to Course

This module is the practical outworking of the Kingdom worldview and leadership foundations established in Modules 1 and 2. While previous modules laid the theological and missional groundwork for a Kingdom business, Module 3 provides the critical ethical and operational framework. It answers the pivotal question: "How does a Kingdom worldview actually change the way I run the day-to-day operations of my business?" The stewardship mandate is the tangible link between the grand vision of advancing God's Kingdom and the granular details of profit margins, payroll, and product sourcing. Mastery of this module's concepts is essential for effectively implementing the strategies for impact and integration that will be covered in subsequent modules.
