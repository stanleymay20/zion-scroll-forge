# Module 1: Foundations of Biblical Economics  
**Course:** Kingdom Economics Foundations  
**Level:** Undergraduate  
**Category:** Economics  

---

## Module Overview  
This module introduces core economic principles through a biblical lens, emphasizing stewardship, divine order, and the integration of faith with economic reasoning. Students will explore how Scripture informs concepts like supply/demand, markets, and human flourishing.  

---

### Learning Objectives  
By the end of this module, students will be able to:  
1. **Define** key economic terms (e.g., scarcity, opportunity cost) using biblical examples.  
2. **Analyze** supply/demand dynamics in light of Proverbs’ wisdom on planning and diligence.  
3. **Evaluate** modern market systems through the lens of stewardship (Genesis 1:28).  
4. **Apply** economic reasoning to personal budgeting aligned with Luke 14:28-30.  
5. **Articulate** how biblical justice intersects with resource allocation (Isaiah 58:10-12).  

---

### Key Concepts Covered  
- **Scarcity and God’s Provision**: Balancing human limitations with divine abundance.  
- **Supply/Demand**: Biblical examples of resource management (e.g., Joseph in Egypt).  
- **Markets as Moral Spaces**: The role of ethics in exchange (Proverbs 11:1).  
- **Stewardship vs. Ownership**: Redefining wealth from a kingdom perspective.  

---

### Scripture Foundations  
1. **Genesis 1:28** – "Fill the earth and subdue it..." (Mandate for stewardship).  
2. **Proverbs 3:9-10** – Honoring God with wealth as a principle of supply.  
3. **Matthew 25:14-30** – Parable of the Talents (Accountability in resource use).  

---

### Real-World Applications  
- Case Study: **Fair Trade Movements** – Aligning ethical consumption with Micah 6:8.  
- Personal Finance: Designing a budget that reflects tithing and savings.  
- Community Impact: Analyzing local food banks through the lens of Acts 4:32-35.  

---

### Module Structure (4 Lectures)  
1. **Lecture 1**: Biblical Frameworks for Economics – From Eden to Exodus.  
   - *Activity*: Compare manna economy (Exodus 16) with modern welfare systems.  
2. **Lecture 2**: Supply/Demand in Scripture – Joseph’s Grain Storage (Genesis 41).  
   - *Activity*: Simulate a market scenario with ethical constraints.  
3. **Lecture 3**: Markets and Morality – Prophetic Critique of Exploitation (Amos 8:4-6).  
   - *Activity*: Debate "Can markets be truly just?"  
4. **Lecture 4**: Stewardship in Practice – From Theory to Household Budgets.  
   - *Activity*: Draft a stewardship covenant for personal finances.  

---

### Assessment Overview  
- **Reflection Journal (20%)**: Responses to prompts connecting lectures to personal stewardship.  
- **Case Study Analysis (30%)**: Evaluate a business’s practices using biblical criteria.  
- **Group Debate (20%)**: Argue for/against "Biblical economics requires wealth redistribution."  
- **Final Project (30%)**: Design a community resource plan grounded in Isaiah 58.  

---

### Estimated Time Commitment  
- **Lectures & Readings**: 6 hours  
- **Activities/Discussions**: 4 hours  
- **Assessments**: 5 hours  
- **Total**: 15 hours  

*Note: Includes "ascension moments" – weekly prayer/reflection on economic choices.*  

---  
**Pedagogy Integration**: Revelation (Scripture) + Reason (economic theory) → Transformation (lived stewardship).
