## Getting Started

### Accessing the System

1. Navigate to https://portal.scrolluniversity.edu
2. Click "Sign In" in the top right corner
3. Enter your university email and password
4. Complete two-factor authentication if prompted

### First-Time Login

On your first login, you'll be guided through:
- Setting up your profile
- Configuring notification preferences
- Reviewing your dashboard
- Completing any required onboarding tasks

### Dashboard Overview

Your dashboard provides quick access to:
- **Upcoming Deadlines**: Important dates and tasks
- **Quick Actions**: Common tasks for your role
- **Notifications**: Recent system messages
- **Progress Tracking**: Your current status and goals

## For Students

### Course Registration

#### Viewing Available Courses

1. Navigate to **Academic** > **Course Catalog**
2. Filter by semester, department, or program
3. Click on a course to view details including:
   - Course description
   - Prerequisites
   - Schedule and location
   - Instructor information
   - Available seats

#### Registering for Courses

1. Go to **Academic** > **Registration**
2. Select the semester you want to register for
3. Search for courses or browse by department
4. Click "Add to Cart" for desired courses
5. Review your schedule for conflicts
6. Click "Submit Registration"

**Note**: The system automatically validates:
- Prerequisites completion
- Schedule conflicts
- Enrollment capacity
- Financial holds

#### Waitlist Management

If a course is full:
1. Click "Join Waitlist" on the course page
2. You'll receive your waitlist position
3. Notifications will be sent if a seat becomes available
4. You'll have 24 hours to accept the seat

### Degree Audit

#### Viewing Your Progress

1. Navigate to **Academic** > **Degree Audit**
2. View your progress dashboard showing:
   - Total credits completed
   - Credits in progress
   - Credits remaining
   - GPA
   - Expected graduation date

#### Understanding Requirements

Your degree audit shows:
- **Core Requirements**: Required courses for your program
- **Electives**: Flexible course options
- **General Education**: University-wide requirements
- **Spiritual Formation**: Faith development requirements

Color coding:
- 🟢 Green: Completed
- 🟡 Yellow: In Progress
- 🔴 Red: Not Started

### Course Learning Experience

#### Accessing Course Materials

1. Go to **My Courses**
2. Click on a course card
3. Navigate through modules in sequential order
4. Access:
   - Video lectures
   - Lecture notes
   - Assignments
   - Quizzes
   - Discussion forums

#### Using the AI Tutor

The AI Tutor (ScrollTutor) is available 24/7 to help you:

1. Click the "Ask AI Tutor" button in any lecture
2. Type your question
3. Receive personalized explanations with:
   - Concept clarification
   - Scripture references
   - Practice problems
   - Related resources

**Tips for effective questions:**
- Be specific about what you don't understand
- Reference the lecture or concept
- Ask follow-up questions for deeper understanding

#### Submitting Assignments

1. Navigate to the assignment in your course
2. Click "Submit Assignment"
3. Upload your file or enter text
4. Review your submission
5. Click "Final Submit"

**Important:**
- Check the deadline before submitting
- You can save drafts before final submission
- Late submissions may incur penalties
- AI-assisted grading provides immediate feedback

### Spiritual Formation

#### Daily Devotions

1. Go to **Spiritual Formation** > **Daily Devotions**
2. Read today's devotion
3. Reflect on the Scripture passage
4. Complete the reflection questions
5. Track your consistency streak

#### Prayer Journal

1. Navigate to **Spiritual Formation** > **Prayer Journal**
2. Click "New Entry"
3. Record your prayers and reflections
4. Tag entries by category
5. Review past entries to see God's faithfulness

#### Prophetic Check-Ins

Quarterly spiritual assessments help track your growth:
1. Complete the questionnaire when prompted
2. Reflect on your spiritual journey
3. Receive personalized recommendations
4. Connect with a spiritual mentor if needed

### Notifications

1. Go to **Settings** > **Notifications**
2. Choose notification channels:
   - Email
   - Push notifications
   - SMS
   - In-app
3. Set preferences by category:
   - Academic deadlines

4. Configure quiet hours

## For Faculty

### Teaching Load Management

#### Viewing Your Assignments

1. Navigate to **Faculty** > **Teaching Load**
2. View your current semester assignments
3. See total credit hours and utilization percentage
4. Review course details and enrollment

#### Requesting Changes

1. Click "Request Change" on your teaching load page
2. Specify the type of change needed
3. Provide justification
4. Submit for department approval

### Content Creation

#### Generating Lecture Plans

The ScrollProfessor AI agent helps create comprehensive lecture plans:

1. Go to **Faculty** > **Content Creation**
2. Select "Generate Lecture Plan"
3. Provide:
   - Course and module information
   - Learning objectives
   - Target duration
   - Student level
4. Review the generated plan
5. Edit and customize as needed
6. Save to your course

**Generated content includes:**
- Detailed outline with timing
- Teaching notes and tips
- Discussion questions
- Spiritual integration points
- Assessment suggestions

#### Creating Assessments

1. Navigate to **Faculty** > **Content Creation**
2. Select "Generate Assessment"
3. Specify:
   - Assessment type (quiz, exam, project)
   - Topics to cover
   - Difficulty level
   - Question count
4. Review generated questions
5. Edit or add questions
6. Create rubric
7. Publish to course

### Grading Automation

#### Using AI-Assisted Grading

1. Go to **Faculty** > **Grading**
2. Select the assignment to grade
3. Click "Auto-Grade Submissions"
4. Review AI-generated grades and feedback
5. Adjust grades flagged for human review
6. Publish grades to students

**AI Grading Features:**
- Confidence scores for each grade
- Detailed feedback generation
- Rubric-based scoring
- Plagiarism detection
- Consistency across submissions

#### Batch Grading

For multiple submissions:
1. Select all submissions to grade
2. Click "Batch Grade"
3. System processes all submissions
4. Review summary report
5. Address any flagged submissions
6. Publish all grades at once

## Troubleshooting

### Cannot Log In

**Problem**: Login fails or account is locked

**Solutions**:
1. Verify you're using your university email
2. Check caps lock is off
3. Try password reset if forgotten
4. Contact IT support if account is locked
5. Ensure two-factor authentication is set up

### Course Not Appearing

**Problem**: Registered course doesn't show in "My Courses"

**Solutions**:
1. Verify registration was successful
2. Check if course has started
3. Refresh your browser
4. Clear browser cache
5. Contact registrar if issue persists

### AI Tutor Not Responding

**Problem**: AI Tutor doesn't answer questions

**Solutions**:
1. Check your internet connection
2. Ensure you're in an active course
3. Try rephrasing your question
4. Refresh the page
5. Report issue if problem continues

### Notifications Not Received

**Problem**: Missing important notifications

**Solutions**:
1. Check notification preferences
2. Verify email address is correct
3. Check spam/junk folder
4. Ensure push notifications are enabled
5. Update notification settings

### Assignment Submission Failed

**Problem**: Cannot submit assignment

**Solutions**:
1. Check file size (max 50MB)
2. Verify file format is accepted
3. Ensure deadline hasn't passed
4. Try different browser
5. Contact instructor if urgent

## FAQs

### General Questions

**Q: How do I know if I'm eligible to register for a course?**  
A: The system automatically checks prerequisites when you attempt to register. You'll see a green checkmark if eligible or a red X with explanation if not.

**Q: Can I drop a course after the add/drop deadline?**  
A: After the add/drop deadline, you can withdraw from a course (which appears on your transcript) until the withdrawal deadline. Contact your advisor for guidance.

**Q: How often is my degree audit updated?**  
A: Your degree audit updates automatically when grades are posted each semester. You can also request a manual refresh.

### For Students

**Q: How do I access the AI Tutor?**  
A: The AI Tutor button appears in every lecture. Click it to start a tutoring session. You can ask questions about the lecture content, request practice problems, or get concept explanations.

**Q: What happens if I'm on a waitlist?**  
A: You'll receive an email and push notification if a seat becomes available. You have 24 hours to accept the seat before it's offered to the next person.

**Q: Can I see my grades before they're officially posted?**  
A: Some instructors use AI-assisted grading which provides immediate feedback. However, all grades must be reviewed and officially posted by the instructor.

### For Faculty

**Q: How accurate is AI-assisted grading?**  
A: AI grading includes a confidence score. High-confidence grades (>90%) are typically very accurate. Lower confidence submissions are automatically flagged for human review.

**Q: Can I customize the AI-generated content?**  
A: Yes! All AI-generated lecture plans, assessments, and materials can be edited and customized to match your teaching style and course needs.

**Q: How do I identify at-risk students?**  
A: The system automatically flags students based on attendance, grades, and engagement. Check your Faculty Dashboard for alerts and recommended interventions.

### For Administrators

**Q: How do I handle workflow failures?**  
A: Failed workflows appear in the Workflow Dashboard with error details. You can retry the workflow, modify it, or manually complete the steps.

**Q: Can I schedule notifications in advance?**  
A: Yes! When creating a notification, select "Schedule for later" and choose the date and time. The system will send it automatically.

**Q: How do I generate reports?**  
A: Navigate to Admin > Reports, select the report type, choose date range and filters, then click "Generate Report". Reports can be exported to PDF or Excel.
