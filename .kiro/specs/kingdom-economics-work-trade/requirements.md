# Requirements Document

## Introduction

The Kingdom Economics & Work-Trade System enables ScrollUniversity to provide debt-free Christian education through value-based pricing, work-trade opportunities, and ministry service credits. This system rejects traditional debt-based education models and implements kingdom principles of stewardship, service, and mutual support to make world-class education accessible to all called students regardless of financial means.

## Glossary

- **Work-Trade Program**: A system where students exchange labor, skills, or services for tuition credits
- **Skills-for-Tuition Exchange**: Marketplace where students offer professional skills in exchange for course credits
- **Ministry Service Credit**: Tuition reduction earned through verified ministry work and kingdom service
- **Value-Based Pricing**: Tuition structure that adjusts based on student's economic context and ability to pay
- **Kingdom-Aligned Financial Aid**: Scholarships and grants based on calling, need, and kingdom impact potential
- **Anti-Debt Enforcement**: System safeguards preventing students from incurring educational debt
- **Stewardship Covenant**: Agreement between student and university regarding financial responsibility and service
- **ScrollGold Integration**: Use of blockchain-based currency for work-trade and service credit transactions

## Requirements

### Requirement 1

**User Story:** As a student with limited financial resources, I want to exchange my professional skills for tuition credits, so that I can afford world-class education without debt.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a student registers skills THEN the system SHALL create a profile listing their professional capabilities and availability
2. WHEN organizations need services THEN the system SHALL match them with qualified students based on skills and schedule
3. WHEN work is completed THEN the system SHALL verify completion and automatically credit tuition to the student's account
4. WHEN calculating credit value THEN the system SHALL use fair market rates for the student's skill level and service type
5. WHEN students lack marketable skills THEN the system SHALL offer training programs that can be exchanged for future tuition

### Requirement 2

**User Story:** As a ministry leader, I want to offer service opportunities that provide tuition credits, so that students can serve the kingdom while funding their education.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN posting ministry opportunities THEN the system SHALL specify service requirements, duration, and tuition credit value
2. WHEN students apply for opportunities THEN the system SHALL match based on calling, skills, and spiritual maturity
3. WHEN service is completed THEN the system SHALL require verification from ministry supervisors before crediting tuition
4. WHEN evaluating service THEN the system SHALL assess both task completion and spiritual growth demonstrated
5. WHEN students serve faithfully THEN the system SHALL provide recommendations and endorsements for future opportunities

### Requirement 3

**User Story:** As a financial aid administrator, I want to implement value-based pricing, so that tuition reflects each student's economic reality while maintaining program sustainability.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a student applies THEN the system SHALL assess their economic context including income, family size, and regional cost of living
2. WHEN calculating tuition THEN the system SHALL adjust pricing to be affordable within the student's economic reality
3. WHEN determining pricing THEN the system SHALL ensure program sustainability through balanced pricing across student population
4. WHEN economic circumstances change THEN the system SHALL allow tuition adjustment without penalty
5. WHEN students from wealthy contexts enroll THEN the system SHALL charge higher tuition to subsidize students with greater need

### Requirement 4

**User Story:** As a student, I want clear visibility into my financial obligations and available funding options, so that I can plan my education without fear of debt.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN viewing financial dashboard THEN the system SHALL display total tuition, credits earned, scholarships, and remaining balance
2. WHEN exploring funding options THEN the system SHALL show available work-trade opportunities, ministry service options, and scholarships
3. WHEN planning enrollment THEN the system SHALL project costs and show pathways to debt-free completion
4. WHEN balance is due THEN the system SHALL prevent debt by requiring payment or approved work-trade commitment before enrollment
5. WHEN students struggle financially THEN the system SHALL proactively suggest additional funding pathways and support

### Requirement 5

**User Story:** As a scholarship coordinator, I want to award kingdom-aligned financial aid, so that students with calling and potential receive support regardless of current resources.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN evaluating scholarship applications THEN the system SHALL assess calling clarity, ministry potential, and kingdom impact likelihood
2. WHEN awarding scholarships THEN the system SHALL prioritize students from underserved regions and unreached people groups
3. WHEN determining award amounts THEN the system SHALL consider both financial need and strategic kingdom priorities
4. WHEN scholarships are awarded THEN the system SHALL require stewardship covenants outlining expectations and accountability
5. WHEN recipients demonstrate faithfulness THEN the system SHALL provide pathways for continued support through degree completion

### Requirement 6

**User Story:** As a system administrator, I want anti-debt enforcement mechanisms, so that no student can incur educational debt through our platform.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN students attempt to enroll THEN the system SHALL verify sufficient funding through payment, work-trade commitment, or approved aid
2. WHEN payment plans are created THEN the system SHALL prohibit interest charges and debt accumulation
3. WHEN students cannot pay THEN the system SHALL pause enrollment rather than allow debt, while preserving progress
4. WHEN external loans are detected THEN the system SHALL warn students and provide debt-free alternatives
5. WHEN students complete programs THEN the system SHALL certify they graduated debt-free as part of their credentials

### Requirement 7

**User Story:** As a student, I want to use ScrollGold for work-trade transactions, so that I have a transparent, blockchain-verified record of my service and earnings.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN work-trade is completed THEN the system SHALL issue ScrollGold to the student's wallet as tuition credit
2. WHEN ScrollGold is earned THEN the system SHALL record the transaction on the blockchain for permanent verification
3. WHEN viewing transaction history THEN the system SHALL display all work-trade earnings with timestamps and verification
4. WHEN converting ScrollGold to tuition THEN the system SHALL apply credits automatically at enrollment
5. WHEN students graduate THEN the system SHALL provide a blockchain-verified record of all work-trade service

### Requirement 8

**User Story:** As an employer, I want to hire ScrollUniversity students for projects, so that I can access skilled labor while supporting their education.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN posting job opportunities THEN the system SHALL specify project requirements, timeline, and compensation in tuition credits
2. WHEN students apply THEN the system SHALL provide verified skill assessments and previous work ratings
3. WHEN hiring students THEN the system SHALL facilitate contracts that protect both parties and ensure fair compensation
4. WHEN work is completed THEN the system SHALL handle payment directly to student tuition accounts
5. WHEN employers are satisfied THEN the system SHALL encourage ongoing relationships and repeat hiring

### Requirement 9

**User Story:** As a student, I want to participate in community work-trade pools, so that I can contribute to collective projects that benefit multiple students.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN community projects are available THEN the system SHALL allow students to join collaborative work-trade initiatives
2. WHEN students contribute to pools THEN the system SHALL track individual contributions and distribute credits fairly
3. WHEN pool projects complete THEN the system SHALL divide earnings based on verified participation and effort
4. WHEN students work together THEN the system SHALL foster community and mutual support among participants
5. WHEN pools are successful THEN the system SHALL create ongoing opportunities for collaborative kingdom work

### Requirement 10

**User Story:** As a financial administrator, I want comprehensive reporting on work-trade and kingdom economics, so that I can demonstrate program effectiveness and sustainability.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN generating reports THEN the system SHALL show total tuition funded through work-trade, ministry service, and scholarships
2. WHEN analyzing sustainability THEN the system SHALL project program viability based on current funding mix and student growth
3. WHEN evaluating impact THEN the system SHALL track students who completed debt-free and their subsequent kingdom impact
4. WHEN reporting to stakeholders THEN the system SHALL demonstrate how kingdom economics enables access for called students
5. WHEN planning expansion THEN the system SHALL model financial scenarios for scaling to new regions and student populations
