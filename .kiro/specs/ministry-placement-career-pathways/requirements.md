# Requirements Document

## Introduction

The Ministry Placement & Career Pathways System connects ScrollUniversity graduates with kingdom opportunities worldwide, ensuring that education translates into measurable kingdom transformation. This system identifies ministry callings, maps career pathways to kingdom roles, integrates marketplace ministry, facilitates partnerships with NGOs and mission organizations, and tracks kingdom impact to fulfill the vision of training "scroll sons to govern nations."

## Glossary

- **Ministry Calling Identification**: Process of discerning God's specific assignment for a student's life and work
- **Career Pathway Mapping**: Connecting educational programs to specific kingdom roles and ministry opportunities
- **Marketplace Ministry**: Integration of faith and professional work in secular employment contexts
- **NGO Partnership**: Collaboration with non-governmental organizations for ministry placement and support
- **Mission Organization**: Faith-based entities focused on evangelism, discipleship, and church planting
- **Government Leadership Placement**: Positioning graduates in governmental roles to influence nations
- **Kingdom Impact Measurement**: Quantifiable and qualitative assessment of transformation resulting from graduate ministry
- **Calling Alignment Score**: Metric indicating how well a career opportunity matches a student's divine calling

## Requirements

### Requirement 1

**User Story:** As a student, I want to identify my specific ministry calling, so that I can pursue education and career paths aligned with God's purposes for my life.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a student begins calling discernment THEN the system SHALL provide comprehensive assessment tools including spiritual gifts, passions, experiences, and prophetic words
2. WHEN assessments are complete THEN the system SHALL generate a calling profile identifying primary and secondary ministry focuses
3. WHEN calling is unclear THEN the system SHALL connect students with calling coaches and provide exploration opportunities
4. WHEN calling evolves THEN the system SHALL allow profile updates while maintaining history of calling journey
5. WHEN calling is confirmed THEN the system SHALL recommend degree programs and courses aligned with that calling

### Requirement 2

**User Story:** As a career counselor, I want to map educational pathways to specific kingdom roles, so that students see clear connections between their studies and future ministry.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN viewing career pathways THEN the system SHALL display kingdom roles organized by ministry focus (missions, church leadership, marketplace, government, etc.)
2. WHEN exploring roles THEN the system SHALL show required education, skills, experience, and spiritual maturity for each pathway
3. WHEN students select pathways THEN the system SHALL generate personalized education plans that prepare them for those roles
4. WHEN pathways are complex THEN the system SHALL break them into achievable milestones with timeline projections
5. WHEN new kingdom roles emerge THEN the system SHALL allow pathway creation and connection to relevant education

### Requirement 3

**User Story:** As a marketplace ministry advocate, I want to help students integrate faith and professional work, so that they see secular employment as valid kingdom calling.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN students pursue marketplace careers THEN the system SHALL provide resources for faith integration in professional contexts
2. WHEN exploring marketplace ministry THEN the system SHALL connect students with mentors successfully living out marketplace calling
3. WHEN students enter workplace THEN the system SHALL offer ongoing support for navigating faith-work integration challenges
4. WHEN marketplace impact occurs THEN the system SHALL document testimonies of kingdom transformation through professional work
5. WHEN students struggle with calling validity THEN the system SHALL provide biblical and practical affirmation of marketplace ministry

### Requirement 4

**User Story:** As an NGO partnership coordinator, I want to connect graduates with mission organizations, so that they find placement in established kingdom work.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN NGOs register THEN the system SHALL create profiles including mission focus, geographic regions, and staffing needs
2. WHEN graduates are ready for placement THEN the system SHALL match them with NGOs based on calling, skills, and cultural fit
3. WHEN matches are made THEN the system SHALL facilitate introductions and provide placement support
4. WHEN graduates join NGOs THEN the system SHALL maintain connection for ongoing support and impact tracking
5. WHEN NGOs provide feedback THEN the system SHALL use it to improve graduate preparation and matching algorithms

### Requirement 5

**User Story:** As a graduate, I want to find ministry opportunities that match my calling and preparation, so that I can immediately begin kingdom impact.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN searching opportunities THEN the system SHALL display positions filtered by calling alignment, location preferences, and qualification match
2. WHEN viewing opportunities THEN the system SHALL show calling alignment scores and explain why positions match or don't match
3. WHEN applying for positions THEN the system SHALL provide verified credentials, skill assessments, and spiritual maturity indicators
4. WHEN opportunities are scarce THEN the system SHALL suggest alternative pathways or entrepreneurial ministry options
5. WHEN graduates are placed THEN the system SHALL provide transition support and ongoing mentorship

### Requirement 6

**User Story:** As a government relations coordinator, I want to position graduates in governmental leadership, so that they influence nations according to kingdom principles.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN students show governmental calling THEN the system SHALL provide specialized preparation in public policy, governance, and kingdom influence
2. WHEN government opportunities arise THEN the system SHALL match qualified graduates with positions at local, national, and international levels
3. WHEN graduates enter government THEN the system SHALL provide ongoing support for maintaining integrity and kingdom values
4. WHEN graduates influence policy THEN the system SHALL document kingdom impact and transformation at governmental levels
5. WHEN networks are needed THEN the system SHALL connect graduates with other believers in government for mutual support

### Requirement 7

**User Story:** As a student, I want to see real examples of graduates living out their calling, so that I can envision my own future kingdom impact.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN exploring pathways THEN the system SHALL display graduate testimonies and case studies for each career path
2. WHEN viewing testimonies THEN the system SHALL show the journey from education through placement to kingdom impact
3. WHEN students need inspiration THEN the system SHALL connect them with graduates in similar callings for mentorship
4. WHEN graduates achieve impact THEN the system SHALL document and share their stories to inspire current students
5. WHEN pathways seem impossible THEN the system SHALL provide examples of God's faithfulness in opening doors

### Requirement 8

**User Story:** As an impact measurement coordinator, I want to track kingdom transformation resulting from graduate ministry, so that we can demonstrate program effectiveness.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN graduates are placed THEN the system SHALL establish baseline metrics for their ministry context
2. WHEN graduates report impact THEN the system SHALL collect quantitative data (people reached, churches planted, policies changed) and qualitative testimonies
3. WHEN measuring transformation THEN the system SHALL assess spiritual, social, economic, and governmental impact
4. WHEN aggregating data THEN the system SHALL show cumulative kingdom impact across all graduates
5. WHEN reporting to stakeholders THEN the system SHALL demonstrate how education translates to measurable kingdom advancement

### Requirement 9

**User Story:** As a student, I want to explore entrepreneurial ministry options, so that I can create kingdom opportunities where none currently exist.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN students have entrepreneurial calling THEN the system SHALL provide resources for starting kingdom businesses and ministries
2. WHEN developing ministry ventures THEN the system SHALL connect students with mentors, funding sources, and support networks
3. WHEN ventures launch THEN the system SHALL provide ongoing coaching and accountability
4. WHEN ventures succeed THEN the system SHALL document models for replication in other contexts
5. WHEN ventures struggle THEN the system SHALL provide troubleshooting support and help discern whether to pivot or persevere

### Requirement 10

**User Story:** As a career services administrator, I want comprehensive analytics on placement and impact, so that I can continuously improve our career pathways system.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN reviewing placement data THEN the system SHALL show placement rates by degree program, calling type, and geographic region
2. WHEN assessing preparation THEN the system SHALL correlate educational experiences with placement success and kingdom impact
3. WHEN identifying gaps THEN the system SHALL highlight areas where graduates struggle to find aligned opportunities
4. WHEN evaluating partnerships THEN the system SHALL assess which NGOs and organizations provide best fit for graduates
5. WHEN planning improvements THEN the system SHALL use data to refine calling identification, pathway mapping, and placement support
