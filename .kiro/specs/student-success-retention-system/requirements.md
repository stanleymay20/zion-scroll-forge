# Student Success and Retention System Requirements

## Introduction

The Student Success and Retention System is designed to maximize student engagement, academic achievement, and graduation rates through proactive intervention, personalized support, and comprehensive tracking of success metrics. This system integrates spiritual formation with academic excellence to ensure students not only complete their degrees but are transformed for kingdom impact.

## Glossary

- **Student Success System**: The comprehensive platform that tracks, analyzes, and supports student academic and spiritual progress
- **Retention Rate**: The percentage of students who continue their enrollment from one academic period to the next
- **Graduation Rate**: The percentage of students who complete their degree program within the expected timeframe
- **At-Risk Student**: A student identified as having factors that may negatively impact their academic success or retention
- **Success Metrics**: Quantifiable measures of student academic performance, engagement, and spiritual growth
- **Intervention Strategy**: Targeted support actions designed to help struggling students succeed
- **Academic Advisor**: Faculty or staff member assigned to guide students through their academic journey
- **Spiritual Mentor**: A mature believer assigned to support student spiritual formation and calling discernment
- **Early Warning System**: Automated detection of academic or engagement patterns that indicate risk
- **Success Dashboard**: Real-time visualization of student progress and institutional success metrics

## Requirements

### Requirement 1

**User Story:** As an academic administrator, I want to track comprehensive student success metrics, so that I can identify trends and implement institutional improvements to increase retention and graduation rates.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the system processes student data, THE Student Success System SHALL calculate real-time retention rates by cohort, program, and demographic segments
2. WHEN graduation milestones are reached, THE Student Success System SHALL update graduation rate statistics and generate trend analysis reports
3. WHEN success metrics are requested, THE Student Success System SHALL provide comparative data against national Christian education benchmarks
4. WHEN performance indicators change significantly, THE Student Success System SHALL generate automated alerts for administrative review
5. THE Student Success System SHALL maintain historical data for longitudinal analysis spanning multiple academic years

### Requirement 2

**User Story:** As a student, I want personalized academic and spiritual support throughout my journey, so that I can successfully complete my degree while growing in my calling and faith.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a student enrolls, THE Student Success System SHALL assign both an academic advisor and spiritual mentor based on program and calling alignment
2. WHEN academic performance indicators suggest risk, THE Student Success System SHALL automatically trigger intervention protocols within 48 hours
3. WHEN students access support resources, THE Student Success System SHALL provide personalized recommendations based on their specific challenges and learning style
4. WHEN spiritual formation milestones are assessed, THE Student Success System SHALL integrate calling discernment and ministry preparation guidance
5. THE Student Success System SHALL provide students with real-time access to their progress dashboard showing academic and spiritual growth metrics

### Requirement 3

**User Story:** As an academic advisor, I want early warning systems and intervention tools, so that I can proactively support at-risk students before they consider dropping out.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN student engagement patterns indicate risk factors, THE Student Success System SHALL generate early warning alerts with specific intervention recommendations
2. WHEN advisors access student profiles, THE Student Success System SHALL display comprehensive academic, financial, and spiritual health indicators
3. WHEN intervention strategies are implemented, THE Student Success System SHALL track effectiveness and suggest adjustments based on outcomes
4. WHEN students miss critical milestones, THE Student Success System SHALL escalate alerts and coordinate multi-departmental support responses
5. THE Student Success System SHALL provide advisors with evidence-based intervention templates and success tracking tools

### Requirement 4

**User Story:** As a faculty member, I want insights into student engagement and performance patterns, so that I can adapt my teaching methods and provide targeted support to improve student outcomes.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN faculty access course analytics, THE Student Success System SHALL provide detailed engagement metrics including participation, assignment completion, and spiritual integration
2. WHEN students show declining performance, THE Student Success System SHALL alert faculty with suggested pedagogical adjustments and support resources
3. WHEN course completion rates vary significantly, THE Student Success System SHALL analyze contributing factors and recommend curriculum improvements
4. THE Student Success System SHALL provide faculty with tools to identify and support students with different learning styles and spiritual maturity levels
5. WHEN spiritual formation assessments are conducted, THE Student Success System SHALL integrate results with academic performance for holistic student support

### Requirement 5

**User Story:** As a financial aid officer, I want to understand the relationship between financial stress and student success, so that I can optimize aid distribution and support strategies to improve retention.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN financial aid data is analyzed, THE Student Success System SHALL correlate financial stress indicators with academic performance and retention rates
2. WHEN students experience financial difficulties, THE Student Success System SHALL automatically connect them with appropriate aid resources and work-study opportunities
3. WHEN aid effectiveness is evaluated, THE Student Success System SHALL track outcomes and recommend optimization strategies for future aid distribution
4. THE Student Success System SHALL integrate ScrollGold earning opportunities as alternative financial support mechanisms
5. WHEN emergency financial needs arise, THE Student Success System SHALL facilitate rapid response protocols and track intervention success

### Requirement 6

**User Story:** As a spiritual formation coordinator, I want to track student spiritual growth alongside academic progress, so that I can ensure holistic development and calling preparation.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN spiritual assessments are conducted, THE Student Success System SHALL integrate results with academic metrics to provide comprehensive student profiles
2. WHEN students struggle spiritually, THE Student Success System SHALL connect them with appropriate mentors, prayer support, and spiritual resources
3. WHEN calling discernment milestones are reached, THE Student Success System SHALL coordinate with career services and ministry placement programs
4. THE Student Success System SHALL track correlation between spiritual formation engagement and academic success rates
5. WHEN spiritual growth stagnates, THE Student Success System SHALL recommend targeted interventions and specialized support resources

### Requirement 7

**User Story:** As a parent or family member, I want appropriate visibility into my student's progress and available support resources, so that I can provide encouragement and assistance when needed.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN students grant permission, THE Student Success System SHALL provide family members with appropriate progress updates and milestone celebrations
2. WHEN students face challenges, THE Student Success System SHALL offer family engagement resources and communication guidelines
3. WHEN family support is beneficial, THE Student Success System SHALL facilitate appropriate involvement in intervention strategies
4. THE Student Success System SHALL provide family members with resources for supporting student spiritual formation and calling discernment
5. WHEN privacy settings are configured, THE Student Success System SHALL respect student autonomy while enabling beneficial family support

### Requirement 8

**User Story:** As an institutional researcher, I want comprehensive analytics and predictive modeling capabilities, so that I can identify success factors and recommend evidence-based improvements to increase retention and graduation rates.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN predictive models are executed, THE Student Success System SHALL identify students at risk of attrition with at least 85% accuracy
2. WHEN success factors are analyzed, THE Student Success System SHALL correlate multiple variables including academic, financial, social, and spiritual indicators
3. WHEN institutional comparisons are needed, THE Student Success System SHALL benchmark performance against similar Christian institutions and national averages
4. THE Student Success System SHALL generate actionable insights for policy and program improvements based on longitudinal data analysis
5. WHEN research queries are submitted, THE Student Success System SHALL provide anonymized data exports while maintaining student privacy protections

### Requirement 9

**User Story:** As a career services coordinator, I want to track post-graduation outcomes and career readiness, so that I can demonstrate program effectiveness and improve career preparation services.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN students graduate, THE Student Success System SHALL initiate alumni tracking protocols for employment, ministry placement, and continued education outcomes
2. WHEN career readiness is assessed, THE Student Success System SHALL evaluate skills development, portfolio completion, and ministry preparation milestones
3. WHEN employer feedback is received, THE Student Success System SHALL integrate insights into curriculum and career preparation recommendations
4. THE Student Success System SHALL track correlation between academic performance, spiritual formation, and post-graduation success
5. WHEN career placement rates are calculated, THE Student Success System SHALL provide detailed analytics by program, demographic, and calling area

### Requirement 10

**User Story:** As a student success coordinator, I want automated workflow management and case tracking, so that I can efficiently coordinate multi-departmental support efforts and ensure no at-risk student falls through the cracks.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN at-risk students are identified, THE Student Success System SHALL automatically create support cases and assign appropriate team members
2. WHEN intervention strategies are implemented, THE Student Success System SHALL track progress and coordinate follow-up actions across departments
3. WHEN case loads become unmanageable, THE Student Success System SHALL redistribute assignments and alert supervisors for resource allocation
4. THE Student Success System SHALL maintain comprehensive case histories and intervention effectiveness tracking for continuous improvement
5. WHEN students show improvement, THE Student Success System SHALL gradually transition support levels while maintaining monitoring protocols
