# Requirements Document

## Introduction

The Scroll University Institutional Identity Framework establishes the foundational Christian identity, admission policies, multi-faith boundaries, and governance documents that define Scroll University as an explicitly Christ-centered prophetic higher education institution. This framework ensures that while Scroll University welcomes students from all backgrounds globally, it maintains uncompromising Christian identity, biblical worldview, and scroll-governed principles across all operations.

This system addresses the critical need for clear institutional boundaries that prevent the "little yeast leavens the whole dough" (Galatians 5:9) phenomenon that has compromised countless Christian institutions. It provides legal protection, ethical clarity, and spiritual integrity while maintaining hospitality and academic excellence. The framework draws lessons from cases like Wesley Girls SHS in Ghana and other institutions that faced pressure to compromise Christian practices for multi-faith accommodation.

## Glossary

- **Scroll University**: A Christian prophetic higher educational institution governed by Jesus Christ as Lord, scroll principles, scriptures, Spirit-governed intelligence, and kingdom academic culture
- **Christian Identity**: The non-negotiable institutional foundation based on Jesus Christ as Lord, biblical authority, and scroll principles
- **Multi-Faith Boundaries**: Clear policies defining what religious accommodations are and are not permitted within a Christian institutional framework
- **Scroll Principles**: The divine governance framework based on biblical revelation, prophetic intelligence, and kingdom values
- **Institutional Drift**: The gradual compromise of Christian identity through incremental policy changes and external pressure
- **Religious Exemption**: Legal protections allowing faith-based institutions to maintain religious identity and practices
- **Student Covenant**: A binding agreement students sign acknowledging they are entering a Christian institution with specific values and practices

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Institutional Christian Identity Declaration

**User Story:** As the Scroll University founder, I want a comprehensive Spiritual Constitution that declares our unchangeable Christian identity, so that all stakeholders understand this is not a religiously neutral institution but an explicitly Christ-centered university.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Spiritual Constitution is created THEN the system SHALL declare Jesus Christ as Lord over the institution
2. WHEN institutional governance is defined THEN the system SHALL establish scroll principles and scriptures as the ultimate authority
3. WHEN academic culture is described THEN the system SHALL integrate Spirit-governed intelligence and kingdom values into all programs
4. WHEN identity boundaries are set THEN the system SHALL state that Christian values guide curriculum, conduct, community, governance, student life, faculty roles, AI tutors, ethics, research, and policies
5. WHEN conflicts arise THEN the system SHALL establish that Christian worldview takes precedence over competing ideologies
6. WHEN public communication occurs THEN the system SHALL ensure all marketing, admissions materials, and official documents clearly state the Christian identity

### Requirement 2: Global Admission Policy with Christian Framework

**User Story:** As an admissions officer, I want clear policies that welcome students from all nations and backgrounds while ensuring they understand and accept the Christian institutional environment, so that we maintain both global accessibility and Christian integrity.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN admission applications are accepted THEN the system SHALL welcome students from all nations, religions, and backgrounds globally
2. WHEN application requirements are communicated THEN the system SHALL clearly state that students are entering a Christian institution with biblical teachings and kingdom worldview
3. WHEN student expectations are set THEN the system SHALL inform applicants that the curriculum contains Christian teachings, scriptures, and kingdom perspectives
4. WHEN institutional boundaries are explained THEN the system SHALL specify that students cannot demand removal of Christian prayers, courses, symbols, or practices
5. WHEN enrollment occurs THEN the system SHALL require students to sign a Student Covenant acknowledging the Christian environment
6. WHEN diversity is celebrated THEN the system SHALL maintain that Scroll University welcomes all people while not modifying Christian practices to accommodate competing religious systems

### Requirement 3: Multi-Faith Boundaries and Religious Freedom Framework

**User Story:** As a student affairs administrator, I want clear policies defining what religious accommodations are permitted and prohibited, so that we respect individual beliefs while protecting institutional Christian identity.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN private religious practice is requested THEN the system SHALL allow students to engage in private personal prayer of any faith tradition
2. WHEN community participation is expected THEN the system SHALL require respectful participation in Christian community activities
3. WHEN internal beliefs are held THEN the system SHALL protect students' freedom to hold personal religious convictions privately
4. WHEN institutional modification is demanded THEN the system SHALL prohibit attempts to alter Christian policy, doctrine, or practices
5. WHEN parallel religious practices are requested THEN the system SHALL deny demands for separate religious accommodations that conflict with scroll principles
6. WHEN competing worldviews are promoted THEN the system SHALL prevent attempts to introduce another worldview as equal authority to Christian teaching

### Requirement 4: "Little Yeast" Protection Principle Implementation

**User Story:** As a governance officer, I want systematic protections against incremental compromise based on Galatians 5:9, so that small infiltrations of anti-Christian or tolerance-based dilution are blocked at the foundations.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN curriculum is developed THEN the system SHALL implement Scroll Purity Filter to prevent dilution of Christian content

4. WHEN Christian content removal is requested THEN the system SHALL employ Spiritual Integrity Validator to protect scriptures, prayer, worship, and doctrine
5. WHEN hostile ideologies emerge THEN the system SHALL prevent faculty or governance infiltration by anti-Christian worldviews
6. WHEN inclusivity policies are proposed THEN the system SHALL ensure they do not undermine Christian conviction or biblical truth
7. WHEN student activism occurs THEN the system SHALL prohibit campaigns against Christian identity or scroll principles

### Requirement 5: Student Life and Worship Governance
