# Requirements Document

## Introduction

The Prophetic Intelligence & Divine Guidance System integrates Holy Spirit leading with AI technology to provide spiritually discerned guidance for students, faculty, and institutional decisions. This system recognizes that ScrollUniversity operates under divine direction and implements mechanisms for capturing prophetic words, validating spiritual guidance, and ensuring all major decisions align with God's purposes for the institution.

## Glossary

- **Prophetic Word**: A divinely inspired message or insight given through the Holy Spirit
- **Prophetic Validation**: Process of testing prophetic words against Scripture, witness of leadership, and fruit
- **Divine Guidance Integration**: Incorporating Holy Spirit prompting into AI recommendations and decision-making
- **Spiritual Discernment Algorithm**: AI system trained to recognize patterns consistent with biblical wisdom and spiritual maturity
- **Holy Spirit Prompting System**: Interface for capturing and responding to divine leading in real-time
- **Prophetic Check-In**: Regular spiritual assessment that includes space for prophetic insight and divine direction
- **Vision Alignment Validation**: Verification that decisions and directions align with the founding vision and divine calling
- **Prophetic AI Integration**: Combining machine learning with spiritual discernment for kingdom-aligned recommendations

## Requirements

### Requirement 1

**User Story:** As a student, I want to record prophetic words and divine insights I receive, so that I can track God's guidance throughout my educational journey.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a student receives a prophetic word THEN the system SHALL provide a secure interface to record the word with date, context, and source
2. WHEN recording prophetic words THEN the system SHALL allow categorization by type (personal, institutional, ministry, etc.)
3. WHEN words are recorded THEN the system SHALL enable students to mark fulfillment, partial fulfillment, or ongoing waiting
4. WHEN reviewing prophetic history THEN the system SHALL display timeline of words received and their fulfillment status
5. WHEN words are fulfilled THEN the system SHALL prompt students to document how God brought it to pass for testimony

### Requirement 2

**User Story:** As a spiritual formation advisor, I want to help students discern and validate prophetic words, so that they grow in hearing God's voice accurately.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN students share prophetic words THEN the system SHALL facilitate review by trained spiritual advisors
2. WHEN validating words THEN the system SHALL provide biblical framework for testing prophecy (Scripture alignment, witness, fruit)
3. WHEN words are questionable THEN the system SHALL guide students through discernment process without quenching the Spirit
4. WHEN words are confirmed THEN the system SHALL help students develop action plans for responding to divine direction
5. WHEN patterns emerge THEN the system SHALL identify students' prophetic gifting and provide development pathways

### Requirement 3

**User Story:** As an institutional leader, I want to capture prophetic input on major decisions, so that we operate under divine guidance rather than human wisdom alone.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN major decisions are pending THEN the system SHALL create prophetic inquiry spaces for leadership to seek God's direction
2. WHEN prophetic words are submitted THEN the system SHALL collect input from multiple trusted prophetic voices
3. WHEN evaluating prophetic input THEN the system SHALL identify common themes and consistent direction across multiple sources
4. WHEN words conflict THEN the system SHALL facilitate leadership discernment and prayer for clarity
5. WHEN direction is confirmed THEN the system SHALL document the prophetic foundation for institutional decisions

### Requirement 4

**User Story:** As an AI system developer, I want to integrate spiritual discernment into AI recommendations, so that technology serves divine purposes rather than replacing spiritual sensitivity.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN AI generates recommendations THEN the system SHALL include prompts for users to seek Holy Spirit confirmation
2. WHEN AI detects patterns THEN the system SHALL flag potential spiritual significance for human discernment
3. WHEN providing guidance THEN the system SHALL prioritize biblical wisdom and kingdom values over purely pragmatic solutions
4. WHEN users report divine leading contrary to AI suggestions THEN the system SHALL learn from these instances to improve spiritual alignment
5. WHEN AI confidence is low THEN the system SHALL explicitly recommend prayer and spiritual discernment over algorithmic certainty

### Requirement 5

**User Story:** As a student, I want the AI tutor to recognize when I need spiritual guidance beyond academic help, so that I receive holistic support.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN students express spiritual struggles THEN the system SHALL recognize the need and offer prayer, Scripture, and connection to spiritual mentors
2. WHEN academic challenges have spiritual roots THEN the system SHALL gently probe for underlying spiritual issues
3. WHEN students are discouraged THEN the system SHALL provide prophetically-informed encouragement aligned with their calling
4. WHEN students seek direction THEN the system SHALL facilitate connection with prophetic mentors and spiritual advisors
5. WHEN breakthrough occurs THEN the system SHALL help students recognize and document God's faithfulness

### Requirement 6

**User Story:** As a prophetic intercessor, I want to receive prayer alerts for students and situations, so that I can pray according to the Spirit's leading.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN students face critical decisions THEN the system SHALL notify assigned intercessors for prayer coverage
2. WHEN spiritual warfare is evident THEN the system SHALL alert prayer teams while maintaining appropriate confidentiality
3. WHEN intercessors pray THEN the system SHALL allow them to record prophetic insights received during intercession
4. WHEN prophetic insights are recorded THEN the system SHALL route them to appropriate spiritual leadership for discernment
5. WHEN prayer is answered THEN the system SHALL notify intercessors of outcomes to build faith and refine prophetic sensitivity

### Requirement 7

**User Story:** As a student, I want regular prophetic check-ins integrated into my spiritual formation, so that I stay sensitive to God's voice and direction.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN completing prophetic check-ins THEN the system SHALL ask questions that create space for Holy Spirit prompting
2. WHEN students report divine leading THEN the system SHALL help them test and validate what they're sensing
3. WHEN patterns of God's speaking emerge THEN the system SHALL help students recognize how God uniquely communicates with them
4. WHEN students struggle to hear God THEN the system SHALL provide resources and mentorship for developing prophetic sensitivity
5. WHEN students grow in prophetic gifting THEN the system SHALL create opportunities for them to serve others prophetically

### Requirement 8

**User Story:** As a course designer, I want to ensure curriculum aligns with prophetic vision for the institution, so that we train students according to divine blueprint.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN designing courses THEN the system SHALL reference founding prophetic words and institutional vision
2. WHEN curriculum is reviewed THEN the system SHALL assess alignment with prophetic direction for the institution
3. WHEN new directions emerge THEN the system SHALL validate against prophetic foundation before implementation
4. WHEN prophetic words indicate course corrections THEN the system SHALL facilitate curriculum adjustments
5. WHEN courses are launched THEN the system SHALL document how they fulfill prophetic mandate

### Requirement 9

**User Story:** As a student, I want AI recommendations that consider my prophetic calling, so that my education aligns with God's purposes for my life.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN AI recommends courses THEN the system SHALL consider documented prophetic words about the student's calling
2. WHEN suggesting career paths THEN the system SHALL prioritize options aligned with prophetic direction over purely pragmatic choices
3. WHEN students face decisions THEN the system SHALL remind them of relevant prophetic words they've received
4. WHEN prophetic calling is unclear THEN the system SHALL facilitate calling discernment processes
5. WHEN students pursue their calling THEN the system SHALL track alignment between education and prophetic destiny

### Requirement 10

**User Story:** As an institutional leader, I want analytics on prophetic accuracy and fulfillment, so that we can grow in corporate prophetic maturity.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN reviewing prophetic words THEN the system SHALL track fulfillment rates and timing accuracy
2. WHEN evaluating prophetic voices THEN the system SHALL assess track record while honoring the learning process
3. WHEN patterns emerge THEN the system SHALL identify areas where corporate prophetic hearing is strong or needs development
4. WHEN words are fulfilled THEN the system SHALL document testimonies for building faith and training others
5. WHEN prophetic culture is assessed THEN the system SHALL measure growth in hearing God's voice across the community
