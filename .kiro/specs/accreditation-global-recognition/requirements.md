# Requirements Document

## Introduction

The Accreditation & Global Recognition System establishes ScrollUniversity as a legitimate, globally-recognized institution of higher education. This system manages the complex process of obtaining and maintaining accreditation from multiple bodies, ensures credential recognition across borders, and builds partnerships with traditional institutions, employers, and governments worldwide. The system is foundational to ScrollUniversity's mission of providing a credible alternative to traditional education while maintaining kingdom-focused values.

## Glossary

- **ScrollUniversity**: The Christian educational platform providing kingdom-focused higher education
- **Accreditation Body**: An organization that evaluates and certifies educational institutions (e.g., TRACS, ABHE, regional accreditors)
- **Credential**: A degree, certificate, or badge issued by ScrollUniversity
- **Recognition Agreement**: A formal agreement with another institution or government to recognize ScrollUniversity credentials
- **Transfer Credit**: Academic credit accepted by another institution for courses completed at ScrollUniversity
- **Articulation Agreement**: A formal agreement defining how credits transfer between institutions
- **Compliance Report**: Documentation demonstrating adherence to accreditation standards
- **Self-Study**: A comprehensive institutional evaluation required for accreditation
- **Site Visit**: An on-site or virtual evaluation by accreditation reviewers
- **Employer Partner**: An organization that recognizes ScrollUniversity credentials for hiring
- **Government Ministry**: A national education authority that recognizes ScrollUniversity
- **Quality Assurance Framework**: The system ensuring educational standards are maintained

## Requirements

### Requirement 1

**User Story:** As a university administrator, I want to manage the accreditation process with multiple accrediting bodies, so that ScrollUniversity gains legitimate recognition and can serve students globally.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN an administrator initiates an accreditation application, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL create a structured workflow with all required documentation, timelines, and compliance checkpoints
2. WHEN accreditation standards are updated by an accrediting body, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL notify relevant stakeholders and update compliance tracking requirements
3. WHEN a self-study document is generated, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL compile data from all institutional systems including enrollment, faculty qualifications, financial health, learning outcomes, and spiritual formation metrics
4. WHEN a site visit is scheduled, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL prepare all required documentation, coordinate logistics, and provide reviewers with secure access to institutional data
5. WHEN accreditation status changes, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL update all public-facing materials, student records, and partner notifications within 24 hours

### Requirement 2

**User Story:** As a prospective student, I want to verify ScrollUniversity's accreditation status and credential recognition, so that I can make an informed decision about enrollment.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a prospective student views the accreditation page, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL display current accreditation status, accrediting body details, accreditation dates, and scope of accreditation
2. WHEN a student searches for credential recognition in a specific country, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL provide information about government recognition, employer acceptance, and transfer credit agreements in that country
3. WHEN a student requests verification of accreditation, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL generate a digitally-signed verification document with blockchain-backed authenticity
4. WHEN accreditation information is displayed, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL include links to independent verification on accrediting body websites
5. WHEN a student compares ScrollUniversity to traditional institutions, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL provide transparent information about accreditation equivalency and any limitations

### Requirement 3

**User Story:** As a faculty member, I want to ensure my courses meet accreditation standards, so that student learning is recognized and transferable.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a faculty member designs a course, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL validate that learning outcomes align with accreditation body requirements and industry standards
2. WHEN course content is created, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL check that rigor levels, assessment methods, and credit hour calculations meet accreditation standards
3. WHEN a course is submitted for approval, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL verify that faculty qualifications meet accreditation requirements for teaching that course
4. WHEN learning outcomes are assessed, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL collect data in formats required for accreditation reporting
5. WHEN accreditation standards change, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL flag courses requiring updates and provide guidance for compliance

### Requirement 4

**User Story:** As a student, I want my ScrollUniversity credits to transfer to other institutions, so that I can continue my education or pursue additional degrees.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a student requests a transfer credit evaluation, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL provide an official transcript with course descriptions, learning outcomes, and accreditation information formatted for transfer evaluation
2. WHEN a transfer credit agreement exists with another institution, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL automatically generate transfer credit recommendations based on articulation agreements
3. WHEN a student completes a course, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL ensure the course record includes all information required by transfer institutions including contact hours, assessment methods, and learning outcomes
4. WHEN a transfer credit request is denied by another institution, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL collect feedback and adjust course documentation to improve future transfer success
5. WHEN articulation agreements are established, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL maintain a searchable database of transfer pathways and credit equivalencies

### Requirement 5

**User Story:** As an employer, I want to verify the legitimacy of ScrollUniversity credentials, so that I can confidently hire graduates.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN an employer requests credential verification, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL provide instant verification through a secure portal with graduate consent
2. WHEN an employer views a ScrollBadge NFT, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL display accreditation information, learning outcomes, and competencies verified through blockchain
3. WHEN an employer partnership is established, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL create a recognition agreement specifying which credentials are accepted for hiring
4. WHEN an employer searches for graduates, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL provide filtering by accredited programs, competencies, and spiritual formation achievements
5. WHEN credential fraud is suspected, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL provide tamper-proof verification through blockchain and digital signatures

### Requirement 6

**User Story:** As a government education ministry official, I want to evaluate ScrollUniversity for national recognition, so that students in my country can enroll with confidence.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a government ministry requests institutional information, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL generate a comprehensive report including accreditation status, quality assurance processes, learning outcomes data, and financial stability
2. WHEN a recognition agreement is established with a government, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL update student eligibility, visa documentation support, and credential recognition information for that country
3. WHEN government standards are updated, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL assess compliance and notify administrators of required changes
4. WHEN a government conducts an audit, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL provide secure access to all required documentation and data
5. WHEN recognition status changes in a country, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL notify affected students and update enrollment eligibility within 48 hours

### Requirement 7

**User Story:** As a university administrator, I want to maintain continuous compliance with accreditation standards, so that ScrollUniversity retains its accredited status.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN compliance data is collected, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL automatically aggregate metrics from all institutional systems including student outcomes, faculty qualifications, financial health, and spiritual formation
2. WHEN a compliance threshold is not met, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL alert administrators and create an improvement plan with specific actions and timelines
3. WHEN annual reports are due to accrediting bodies, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL generate reports with all required data, evidence, and narrative responses
4. WHEN substantive changes occur (new programs, locations, delivery methods), THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL initiate the appropriate approval process with accrediting bodies
5. WHEN accreditation renewal is approaching, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL create a timeline with all required tasks, assign responsibilities, and track completion

### Requirement 8

**User Story:** As a student, I want to understand how ScrollUniversity's spiritual formation integrates with accreditation, so that I know my faith-based education is academically rigorous.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a student views program information, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL explain how spiritual formation components meet accreditation standards for religious institutions
2. WHEN spiritual formation is assessed, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL document outcomes in ways that satisfy both spiritual and academic accreditation requirements
3. WHEN a student transfers to a secular institution, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL provide clear documentation of which spiritual formation credits may transfer as electives or general education
4. WHEN accreditation bodies review spiritual formation, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL demonstrate measurable outcomes, assessment methods, and integration with academic learning
5. WHEN students complete spiritual formation requirements, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL issue credentials that are recognized by both religious and secular accrediting bodies

### Requirement 9

**User Story:** As a university administrator, I want to build a network of institutional partnerships, so that ScrollUniversity students have pathways to traditional institutions and vice versa.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a partnership opportunity is identified, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL create a partnership proposal including accreditation information, program comparisons, and proposed articulation agreements
2. WHEN an articulation agreement is negotiated, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL map course equivalencies, credit transfer rules, and admission requirements
3. WHEN a partnership is established, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL maintain the agreement, track student transfers, and monitor success rates
4. WHEN partnership agreements expire, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL initiate renewal processes and update agreements based on program changes
5. WHEN students inquire about transfer pathways, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL provide personalized transfer plans based on existing partnerships and articulation agreements

### Requirement 10

**User Story:** As a quality assurance officer, I want to monitor institutional effectiveness, so that ScrollUniversity maintains high standards and continuous improvement.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN learning outcomes are assessed, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL collect data across all programs and compare results against accreditation benchmarks and peer institutions
2. WHEN quality metrics fall below thresholds, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL trigger improvement processes and track remediation efforts
3. WHEN accreditation bodies require evidence of continuous improvement, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL generate reports showing assessment cycles, improvements implemented, and outcomes achieved
4. WHEN new quality standards are adopted, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL update assessment processes and train faculty on new requirements
5. WHEN external reviews are conducted, THE ScrollUniversity System SHALL provide comprehensive evidence of quality assurance processes and outcomes
