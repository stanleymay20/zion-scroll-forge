# Marketing & Growth System Requirements

## Introduction

The Marketing & Growth System is the strategic foundation for acquiring ScrollUniversity's first 100 students by Summer 2026 launch. This system encompasses brand positioning, lead generation, content marketing, conversion optimization, and student retention strategies specifically designed for the Christian higher education market. Unlike operational automation (handled by Zapier), this system defines WHAT to market, WHO to target, and HOW to position ScrollUniversity in a competitive landscape.

## Glossary

- **Marketing System**: The integrated platform and strategy for attracting, converting, and retaining students
- **Lead**: A prospective student who has expressed interest through form submission, content download, or inquiry
- **Conversion Funnel**: The journey from awareness to enrollment: Awareness → Interest → Decision → Action → Retention
- **MQL (Marketing Qualified Lead)**: A lead who has engaged with content and meets basic enrollment criteria
- **SQL (Sales Qualified Lead)**: An MQL who has requested information about enrollment or application
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)**: Total marketing spend divided by number of enrolled students
- **LTV (Lifetime Value)**: Total revenue expected from a student over their entire relationship with ScrollUniversity
- **Organic Traffic**: Website visitors from unpaid sources (SEO, social media, referrals)
- **Paid Traffic**: Website visitors from paid advertising (Google Ads, Facebook Ads, etc.)
- **Content Marketing**: Creating valuable content to attract and engage target audience
- **SEO (Search Engine Optimization)**: Optimizing content to rank higher in search engine results
- **Conversion Rate**: Percentage of visitors who complete a desired action (form submission, application, enrollment)
- **Retention Rate**: Percentage of enrolled students who continue their studies
- **Referral Program**: System for encouraging current students to recommend ScrollUniversity to others
- **Brand Positioning**: How ScrollUniversity is perceived relative to competitors in the market
- **Value Proposition**: The unique benefits ScrollUniversity offers that competitors don't

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Brand Identity & Positioning

**User Story:** As a prospective student, I want to immediately understand what makes ScrollUniversity unique, so that I can determine if it's the right fit for my educational and spiritual goals.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a visitor lands on the homepage THEN the system SHALL display a clear value proposition within 3 seconds that communicates "Kingdom-focused AI-powered Christian education"
2. WHEN a visitor explores the website THEN the system SHALL maintain consistent brand voice, visual identity, and messaging across all pages
3. WHEN a visitor compares ScrollUniversity to competitors THEN the system SHALL provide differentiation through unique positioning of spiritual formation + academic excellence + AI technology
4. WHERE brand materials are created THEN the system SHALL follow brand guidelines for logo usage, color palette, typography, and tone of voice
5. WHEN brand messaging is developed THEN the system SHALL align with the Scroll worldview and theological foundations documented in steering files

### Requirement 2: Target Audience Definition & Segmentation

**User Story:** As a marketing strategist, I want to clearly define and segment our target audiences, so that I can create personalized messaging and campaigns for each group.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN defining target audiences THEN the system SHALL identify at least 5 distinct student personas with demographics, psychographics, pain points, and goals
2. WHEN segmenting audiences THEN the system SHALL categorize prospects by: traditional students (18-22), adult learners (23+), missionaries, ministry leaders, and career changers
3. WHEN analyzing audience needs THEN the system SHALL document specific pain points for each segment (e.g., affordability, flexibility, spiritual depth, career outcomes)
4. WHERE audience research is conducted THEN the system SHALL gather data from surveys, interviews, and competitive analysis
5. WHEN creating marketing materials THEN the system SHALL tailor messaging to address the specific needs and motivations of each audience segment

### Requirement 3: Website Optimization & Conversion

**User Story:** As a prospective student, I want a fast, intuitive website experience that helps me find information and take action, so that I can easily explore programs and apply.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a visitor accesses the website THEN the system SHALL load the homepage in under 2 seconds on desktop and mobile
2. WHEN a visitor navigates the site THEN the system SHALL provide clear calls-to-action (CTA) on every page leading to inquiry forms, course previews, or application
3. WHEN a visitor submits a form THEN the system SHALL capture lead information and trigger automated follow-up within 5 minutes
4. WHERE conversion optimization is implemented THEN the system SHALL A/B test headlines, CTAs, form fields, and page layouts to improve conversion rates
5. WHEN tracking website performance THEN the system SHALL monitor bounce rate, time on site, pages per session, and conversion rate by traffic source

### Requirement 4: Content Marketing Strategy

**User Story:** As a content marketer, I want to create valuable educational content that attracts organic traffic and establishes thought leadership, so that we build trust and authority in Christian education.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN developing content strategy THEN the system SHALL publish at least 2 blog posts per week on topics relevant to Christian education, AI in learning, and spiritual formation
2. WHEN creating content THEN the system SHALL optimize each piece for SEO with target keywords, meta descriptions, internal links, and structured data
3. WHEN distributing content THEN the system SHALL share across multiple channels: blog, email newsletter, social media, and partner websites
4. WHERE content types are selected THEN the system SHALL include: blog posts, video content, infographics, case studies, student testimonials, and downloadable resources
5. WHEN measuring content performance THEN the system SHALL track organic traffic, keyword rankings, social shares, and lead generation from each content piece

### Requirement 5: Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

**User Story:** As a prospective student searching online, I want to find ScrollUniversity when I search for Christian online education, so that I can discover this option for my studies.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN implementing technical SEO THEN the system SHALL ensure proper site structure, XML sitemap, robots.txt, canonical tags, and schema markup
2. WHEN optimizing on-page SEO THEN the system SHALL target primary keywords: "Christian online university", "AI-powered Christian education", "biblical higher education", "kingdom-focused degrees"
3. WHEN building backlinks THEN the system SHALL acquire at least 50 high-quality backlinks from Christian education sites, ministry blogs, and educational directories within 6 months
4. WHERE local SEO is relevant THEN the system SHALL optimize for location-based searches if physical presence exists
5. WHEN tracking SEO performance THEN the system SHALL monitor keyword rankings, organic traffic growth, domain authority, and page authority monthly

### Requirement 6: Social Media Marketing

**User Story:** As a social media manager, I want to build engaged communities on relevant platforms, so that we can reach prospective students where they spend time online.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN establishing social presence THEN the system SHALL create and maintain active profiles on Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, and Twitter
2. WHEN posting content THEN the system SHALL publish at least 5 times per week with a mix of educational content, student stories, spiritual insights, and program information
3. WHEN engaging with audience THEN the system SHALL respond to comments and messages within 24 hours
4. WHERE social advertising is used THEN the system SHALL run targeted campaigns to specific audience segments with clear conversion goals
5. WHEN measuring social performance THEN the system SHALL track follower growth, engagement rate, click-through rate, and leads generated from each platform

### Requirement 7: Email Marketing & Lead Nurture

**User Story:** As a prospective student who downloaded a resource, I want to receive helpful information about ScrollUniversity over time, so that I can make an informed decision about enrolling.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a lead is captured THEN the system SHALL automatically enroll them in a nurture sequence based on their source and interests
2. WHEN creating email sequences THEN the system SHALL develop at least 3 nurture tracks: general inquiry, program-specific, and application-started
3. WHEN sending emails THEN the system SHALL personalize content based on lead data (name, program interest, engagement history)
4. WHERE email frequency is determined THEN the system SHALL send 1-2 emails per week to avoid overwhelming leads while maintaining engagement
5. WHEN measuring email performance THEN the system SHALL track open rate, click-through rate, unsubscribe rate, and conversion to application

### Requirement 8: Paid Advertising Strategy

**User Story:** As a growth marketer, I want to run targeted paid advertising campaigns, so that we can quickly reach qualified prospects and accelerate student acquisition.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN launching paid campaigns THEN the system SHALL run ads on Google Search, Google Display Network, Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn
2. WHEN targeting audiences THEN the system SHALL use demographic, interest, and behavioral targeting to reach Christian students interested in online education
3. WHEN setting budgets THEN the system SHALL allocate spend based on channel performance with target CAC under $500 per enrolled student
4. WHERE ad creative is developed THEN the system SHALL A/B test headlines, images, copy, and CTAs to optimize performance
5. WHEN measuring ad performance THEN the system SHALL track impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, conversion rate, and ROAS (return on ad spend)

### Requirement 9: Referral & Word-of-Mouth Programs

**User Story:** As a current student, I want to easily refer friends and family to ScrollUniversity, so that I can help others discover this opportunity and potentially earn rewards.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN implementing referral program THEN the system SHALL provide each student with a unique referral link and tracking code
2. WHEN a referral converts THEN the system SHALL reward both referrer and new student with ScrollGold, tuition credit, or other incentives
3. WHEN promoting referral program THEN the system SHALL communicate benefits through email, student portal, and social media
4. WHERE testimonials are collected THEN the system SHALL gather video and written testimonials from satisfied students for marketing use
5. WHEN measuring referral performance THEN the system SHALL track referral link clicks, applications from referrals, and enrollment conversion rate

### Requirement 10: Partnership & Affiliate Marketing

**User Story:** As a partnership manager, I want to establish relationships with churches, ministries, and Christian organizations, so that we can reach students through trusted community channels.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN identifying partners THEN the system SHALL target churches, mission organizations, Christian colleges, and ministry networks
2. WHEN creating partnership agreements THEN the system SHALL offer co-marketing opportunities, tuition discounts for partner members, and affiliate commissions
3. WHEN supporting partners THEN the system SHALL provide marketing materials, co-branded landing pages, and dedicated support contacts
4. WHERE affiliate program exists THEN the system SHALL track referrals, provide real-time reporting, and automate commission payments
5. WHEN measuring partnership performance THEN the system SHALL track leads, applications, and enrollments from each partner organization

### Requirement 11: Webinar & Event Marketing

**User Story:** As a prospective student, I want to attend live webinars and virtual events, so that I can learn more about ScrollUniversity, ask questions, and meet faculty and students.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN planning events THEN the system SHALL host at least 2 webinars per month on topics like "Choosing a Christian University", "AI in Education", and "Program Overviews"
2. WHEN promoting events THEN the system SHALL use email, social media, paid ads, and partner channels to drive registrations
3. WHEN hosting webinars THEN the system SHALL include live Q&A, faculty presentations, student testimonials, and clear next steps
4. WHERE event follow-up occurs THEN the system SHALL send recording, resources, and personalized follow-up to all registrants within 24 hours
5. WHEN measuring event performance THEN the system SHALL track registrations, attendance rate, engagement during event, and post-event conversions

### Requirement 12: Student Testimonials & Social Proof

**User Story:** As a prospective student, I want to see real stories from current students and graduates, so that I can trust that ScrollUniversity delivers on its promises.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN collecting testimonials THEN the system SHALL gather video and written testimonials from at least 20 students covering diverse programs and backgrounds
2. WHEN displaying social proof THEN the system SHALL showcase testimonials on homepage, program pages, and throughout the conversion funnel
3. WHEN featuring student stories THEN the system SHALL highlight transformation in academic achievement, spiritual growth, and career outcomes
4. WHERE case studies are created THEN the system SHALL develop detailed success stories with before/after narratives and measurable results
5. WHEN measuring social proof impact THEN the system SHALL A/B test pages with and without testimonials to quantify conversion lift

### Requirement 13: Marketing Analytics & Attribution

**User Story:** As a marketing director, I want comprehensive analytics on all marketing activities, so that I can make data-driven decisions and optimize budget allocation.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN implementing analytics THEN the system SHALL track the complete customer journey from first touch to enrollment
2. WHEN attributing conversions THEN the system SHALL use multi-touch attribution to credit all touchpoints in the conversion path
3. WHEN reporting performance THEN the system SHALL provide weekly dashboards showing traffic, leads, MQLs, SQLs, applications, and enrollments by source
4. WHERE ROI is calculated THEN the system SHALL track spend and revenue by channel to determine which sources deliver best ROI
5. WHEN analyzing data THEN the system SHALL identify trends, anomalies, and opportunities for optimization in real-time

### Requirement 14: Competitive Analysis & Market Intelligence

**User Story:** As a marketing strategist, I want to understand our competitive landscape, so that I can position ScrollUniversity effectively and identify market opportunities.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN conducting competitive analysis THEN the system SHALL identify at least 10 direct and indirect competitors in Christian online education
2. WHEN analyzing competitors THEN the system SHALL evaluate their positioning, pricing, programs, marketing tactics, and student reviews
3. WHEN monitoring market THEN the system SHALL track competitor website changes, new program launches, and marketing campaigns monthly
4. WHERE differentiation is needed THEN the system SHALL identify gaps in competitor offerings that ScrollUniversity can fill
5. WHEN reporting competitive intelligence THEN the system SHALL provide quarterly competitive landscape reports to leadership

### Requirement 15: Marketing Automation & CRM Integration

**User Story:** As a marketing operations manager, I want seamless integration between marketing tools and CRM, so that lead data flows automatically and sales can follow up effectively.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN capturing leads THEN the system SHALL automatically sync lead data to CRM (Supabase) in real-time
2. WHEN scoring leads THEN the system SHALL assign lead scores based on engagement, demographics, and behavior to prioritize follow-up
3. WHEN triggering automations THEN the system SHALL use Zapier to connect marketing tools (Mailchimp, Google Ads, Facebook) with CRM and notification systems
4. WHERE lead routing occurs THEN the system SHALL automatically assign leads to appropriate admissions counselors based on program interest and geography
5. WHEN measuring automation performance THEN the system SHALL track lead response time, follow-up completion rate, and conversion rate by automation

### Requirement 16: Landing Page Optimization

**User Story:** As a paid advertising manager, I want dedicated landing pages for each campaign, so that I can maximize conversion rates and provide relevant experiences.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN creating landing pages THEN the system SHALL build dedicated pages for each major campaign, program, and audience segment
2. WHEN designing landing pages THEN the system SHALL follow best practices: single clear CTA, minimal navigation, compelling headline, social proof, and form above fold
3. WHEN testing landing pages THEN the system SHALL A/B test variations of headlines, images, copy, form fields, and CTAs
4. WHERE mobile optimization is needed THEN the system SHALL ensure landing pages load quickly and display properly on all devices
5. WHEN measuring landing page performance THEN the system SHALL track traffic, bounce rate, time on page, form submissions, and cost per conversion

### Requirement 17: Video Marketing Strategy

**User Story:** As a video content creator, I want to produce engaging video content, so that we can reach visual learners and increase engagement across channels.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN developing video strategy THEN the system SHALL create at least 4 videos per month including program overviews, student testimonials, faculty interviews, and educational content
2. WHEN distributing videos THEN the system SHALL publish on YouTube, embed on website, share on social media, and include in email campaigns
3. WHEN optimizing videos THEN the system SHALL include captions, SEO-optimized titles and descriptions, and clear CTAs
4. WHERE video ads are used THEN the system SHALL run YouTube pre-roll and social video ads targeting prospective students
5. WHEN measuring video performance THEN the system SHALL track views, watch time, engagement rate, click-through rate, and conversions from video traffic

### Requirement 18: Retargeting & Remarketing

**User Story:** As a performance marketer, I want to re-engage visitors who didn't convert, so that we can bring them back and increase overall conversion rates.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN implementing retargeting THEN the system SHALL install tracking pixels for Google, Facebook, and LinkedIn on all website pages
2. WHEN segmenting audiences THEN the system SHALL create retargeting audiences based on pages visited, time on site, and actions taken
3. WHEN creating retargeting ads THEN the system SHALL develop specific creative for each audience segment addressing their stage in the funnel
4. WHERE frequency capping is needed THEN the system SHALL limit ad impressions to avoid overwhelming prospects
5. WHEN measuring retargeting performance THEN the system SHALL track return visits, conversion rate lift, and incremental conversions from retargeting campaigns

### Requirement 19: Student Retention Marketing

**User Story:** As a retention marketer, I want to keep enrolled students engaged and progressing, so that we maximize completion rates and lifetime value.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN students enroll THEN the system SHALL trigger onboarding email sequence welcoming them and providing resources for success
2. WHEN monitoring engagement THEN the system SHALL identify at-risk students based on login frequency, course progress, and assignment completion
3. WHEN re-engaging students THEN the system SHALL send personalized messages encouraging continued progress and offering support resources
4. WHERE community building occurs THEN the system SHALL promote student events, study groups, and social features to increase connection
5. WHEN measuring retention THEN the system SHALL track course completion rate, term-to-term retention, and graduation rate by cohort

### Requirement 20: Launch Campaign Planning

**User Story:** As a launch campaign manager, I want a comprehensive go-to-market plan for Summer 2026, so that we successfully acquire our first 100 students.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN planning launch THEN the system SHALL develop a 6-month pre-launch campaign building awareness and capturing early interest
2. WHEN executing launch THEN the system SHALL coordinate across all channels (content, social, email, paid, PR, partnerships) for maximum impact
3. WHEN creating launch offers THEN the system SHALL provide early-bird tuition discounts, founding student benefits, and limited-time incentives
4. WHERE PR is needed THEN the system SHALL secure coverage in Christian media, education publications, and tech blogs
5. WHEN measuring launch success THEN the system SHALL track applications, enrollments, and achievement of 100-student goal by Summer 2026 deadline
